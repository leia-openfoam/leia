\section{Summary}

\paragraph{The test.} At $\mathbf u=0$ the level-set equation of \texttt{leiaLevelSetFoam} reduces
to $\partial_\tau\psi=\psi\,G(q)$, $q=|\nabla\psi|$, so a run integrates a source alone. A circle
($R=0.25$, centre off the cell lattice) on a uniform 2D mesh, $N=64,128,256,512$,
$\Delta\tau=h/2$, $\tau\le80$ in units of the source time $1/\mu$; eight initial fields with the
same zero set; every gradient-control law (Soga's and Bothe's included), \texttt{beta}, and the
SDPLS strain sources; the source on a band of three cells and on the whole domain. 608 runs, all
complete, every check passed (Section~\ref{sec:checks}).

\paragraph{Results.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \Meas{} The Detrixhe--Aslam volume depends on the profile of $\psi$, not only on its zero
set: $(r^2-R^2)/(2R)$ has twice the volume error of the signed distance $d$, and the difference
converges at order 1.97. A source that restores $d$ therefore changes the measured volume by
$O(h^2)$ without moving the zero set (Table~\ref{tab:repr}).
\item \Meas{} With the centred gradient, every law is unstable at the grid scale. The error grows
exponentially at the rate $G(0)$, the value of the law at $q=0$, within a few percent for every
law (Table~\ref{tab:growth}). At a grid-scale extremum of $\psi$ the centred gradient is zero; on
a staircase (steps of $2h$ every two cells) it is exactly 1, so the source sees a correct profile
and does not correct it.
\item \Meas{} From $d$ in the band, a slow drift (the error doubles every 8 to 9 time units)
precedes the growth; the zero crossings move $0.46h$, $0.30h$, $0.080h$, $0.032h$ at
$N=64$ to 512 by $\tau=80$, order 2.35 in length (Table~\ref{tab:shift}). From every other start
the growth at $G(0)$ arrives earlier (Table~\ref{tab:survband}). On the whole domain every law
fails by $\tau=10$ to 50, from the kink of $d$ at the circle centre (Table~\ref{tab:survnone}).
\item \Meas{} No law restores $d$ from the blends or the algebraic fields without moving the
zero set. The laws that keep the zero set of $d$ (cubic, soft wall) do so because they hardly act
near $q=1$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Consequence.} The defect is the estimate of $q$, not the law. The next step is the
algebraic rescaling of D.~Bothe (Section~\ref{sec:rescaling}): a pointwise map with no
growth mechanism, as an alternative to a source term, tested on this same case first.

\section{The sources, one paragraph each}
\label{sec:each}

$\zeta=(q^2-1)/2$; every rate constant is $\mu=1$.

\begin{description}
\item[\texttt{linearQ}, $G=-(q-1)$ (Soga; Bothe--Fricke--Soga).] Follows the exact zero-set equation
$\dot q=qG(q)$ for $\tau\le2$ (from $r^2-R^2$: 0.7325 against 0.7311 at $\tau=1$), then grows at
0.98 to 1.00 ($G(0)=1$). In the band it keeps $d$ to $\tau=80$ only at $N\ge256$; from every
other start it fails by $\tau=10$ to 80. Whole domain: fails by $\tau=10$.
\item[\texttt{beta}, $G=1-q$.] Bit-identical to \texttt{linearQ} at $\mu=1$ (48 of 48 pairs).
\item[\texttt{linearZ}, $G=-\zeta$.] As \texttt{linearQ}, at the rate 0.46 to 0.50 ($G(0)=0.5$).
\item[\texttt{saturatedLinearZ}, $G=-\zeta/(1+|\zeta|)$.] The slowest growth, 0.331 to 0.337
($G(0)=1/3$); the same failure pattern, later. No far sign flips on the whole domain (as
predicted, $|F|\le1$).
\item[\texttt{twoThirdsZReg}, $G=-\zeta/(\zeta^2+\varepsilon^2)^{1/6}$.] The steepest law at $q=1$
($|G'(1)|=3.68$): removes every zero crossing of $d$ in the band by $\tau=60$ to 80 at
$N\le256$; rate 0.59 to 0.62 ($G(0)=0.63$).
\item[\texttt{cubicQ}, $G=-(q-1)^3$; \texttt{cubicZ}, $G=-\zeta^3$.] In the band they keep the zero
set of $d$ (shift $10^{-9}h$ and below) because $|F|\sim|q-1|^3$ is tiny near $q=1$; for the same
reason they leave the blends and the normalised algebraic field almost unchanged in 80 time
units. From $r^2-R^2$ they correct the profile, and \texttt{cubicZ} then fails by $\tau=40$ to 80.
Whole domain: fail by $\tau=10$ to 50 (rate $\approx G(0)$).
\item[\texttt{softWall}, $G=-\tanh(\gamma((q-1)/\delta)^5)$ (Bothe S1a, $\epsilon_D=0.8$).] Acts only
outside $|q-1|\approx\delta=0.08$: keeps $d$ and the small blends untouched (at $N\ge256$ its source
is below the round-off of the matrix diagonal), and from every start with $|q-1|>\delta$ it grows at
1.00 to 1.02 ($G(0)=1$) and fails by $\tau=10$ to 20. On the whole domain it amplifies round-off:
serial and four-process runs differ by 20\,\%.
\item[\texttt{R}, \texttt{Rdiv}, \texttt{RdivStrictSp} (SDPLS strain sources).] Zero at
$\mathbf u=0$ by construction; bit-identical to no source (48 of 48 pairs). Likewise the strain
half of \texttt{linearQ} with strain weight \texttt{full} (32 of 32).
\end{description}

\section{Checks and pre-registered lines}
\label{sec:checks}

The first cluster run (commit 010b553a) is void: at $\mathbf u=0$ the matrix of $\psi$ is
diagonal, the solver residual is scaled by the whole field, and a small source update started
below the tolerance, so the solver left $\psi$ unchanged in 68\,\% of the solves. The re-run forces
one iteration (\texttt{PSI\_MIN\_ITER 1}); every log is checked: no solve skipped a nonzero
residual (608 of 608). The initial fields equal their formulas to $10^{-14}$ (608 of 608); 132
null pairs are bit-identical. Table~\ref{tab:verdicts} counts the verdicts of the pre-registered
lines (the config headers of \path{config/staticSource/}).

Wrong predictions, stated: P1.1 predicted order 2 for the L2 fraction error over the cut cells;
it is order 1 by construction. P4.2 predicted a zero-crossing shift of $d$ of at least $0.05h$ on
every rung by $\tau=80$; at $N=512$ it is $0.032h$, so the damage from $d$ converges. P5.3
predicted Crank--Nicolson sign flips far from the circle by $\tau=10$ on every mesh; a flip needs
$|F|\Delta\tau>2$, which takes longer on finer meshes. The crossing error zcL2 has order 1.65
because of the edges nearly parallel to the circle (2.03 without them).
