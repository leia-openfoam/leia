# The "coupled loop through the velocity at rate mu" reading of HL1q/HL1z (corrected 2026-09-28)

> CORRECTED 2026-09-28. The claim, in the pre-print of 2026-09-27, was that HL1q and HL1z destroy the stationary droplet at N = 200 through the coupled loop psi to kappa to f to u to psi to q to F at the rate mu = 1/T_ref, the loop that amplified the SDPLS current 260x: "with mu = 1/T_ref the law acts on the time scale of the arm ... in the coupled arms mu is large (270 to 662 1/s), and the stationary droplet fails within 0.035 s" ([gcls pre-print, sec:discussion at 8867581](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L910-L919) `sec:discussion`; the SDPLS 260x in the [SDPLS article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L1234)). The measurement is first a reading of the code: the law is F = w(q^2) a + G(q^2, sigma) ([`gradientControlLaw.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/gradientControl/gradientControlLaw.H#L181-L185)), the strain weight `none` returns w = 0 ([`strainWeight.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/gradientControl/strainWeight.H#L119-L130)), `linearQ` returns -mu (q - 1) and `linearZ` returns -mu (q^2 - 1)/2 with the sigma argument unnamed ([`gradientControlLaws.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/gradientControl/gradientControlLaws.H#L116-L157)), and the source computes the strain tensor only when a law needs it ([`slGradientControlSource.C`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/source/slGradientControlSource.C#L145-L184)). So for HL1q and HL1z the loop is psi to q to F to psi, with no velocity in it. Second, the histories of the archive (N = 200): HL1q's curvature error e-folds at about 290 1/s over 10 to 20 ms (about 460 1/s over 2 to 10 ms) and its spurious current at 320 to 350 1/s, of the order of mu = 270 1/s and far below the mesh-scale capillary rate of about 1e5 1/s; HL1q and HL1z agree to three digits until t = 0.02 s; the curvature error leads the velocity ([technical report 3.3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-technical-report/gclsTechnicalReport.tex#L216-L246), [gcls article, d1e3414](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L761-L779)). The replacement reading: the exponential source with a centred q has an exact eigenmode, the distance-modulated checkerboard delta_c = (-1)^c E d_c, that grows by (1 + mu dt) per step, DERIVED ([technical report 3.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-technical-report/gclsTechnicalReport.tex#L181-L200)); the flow loop is the second stage. Scope: the pre-print's discussion and the note [[concepts/why-the-candidates-failed]] are corrected. No data is void.

## What survives

1. Every measurement of the campaign: the verdicts (HL1q FAIL 0.204 / 22 / 18 / 0 / 0, HL1z FAIL 6.490 / 23 / 19 / 0 / 0), the destroyed stationary droplet at N = 200 (shape 3.8 R and volume 76 % for HL1q; 3.7 R and 84 % for HL1z) ([STATUS 11.15](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L3949-L3953), [L4006-L4013](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L4006-L4013)), see [[studies/method-gate-2d-campaign-2026-09]].
2. The crossing-shift formula, as a secondary O(h) contribution ([technical report 3.5](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-technical-report/gclsTechnicalReport.tex#L265-L270)).
3. The SDPLS loop reading for S1 and HL2 in the coupled arms, where the soft wall reads sigma of the spurious current ([technical report 3.4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-technical-report/gclsTechnicalReport.tex#L257-L258)).
4. The sampler-across-the-jump reading for HL0 on the oscillating arm, as a HYPOTHESIS; on the translating arm the sampler injects the spurious current unprojected, MEASURED ([[concepts/why-the-candidates-failed]]).
5. The cheapest discriminator, which needs no flow: a signed-distance circle with u = 0 and the source on, at mu in {27, 270, 2700} 1/s; the prediction is a growth of max|psi_c - d_c| as exp(mu t) with the pattern (-1)^(i+j) d ([gcls article, d1e3414](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L776-L779)), see [[concepts/gradient-control-next-experiments]].
