# The SDPLS pre-print and decks

> **Verdict (2026-09-28).** The pre-print `sdplsLevelSet.tex` is the written record of the Eulerian source-term line ([title, line 25](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L25); last commit 246c96e, 2026-09-22). Its measured claims stand as the article states them: the strain-cancelling source `R` converges in the band gradient error on non-reversing flows where the sourceless run diverges ([`sec:verification`, line 232](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L232)); coupled to the stationary droplet the same source destroys the droplet with the production curvature and is inert with the exact curvature ([`sec:coupled-headline`, line 1127](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L1127)); the divergence form `Rdiv` is withdrawn as a method ([`sec:rdiv-mechanism`, line 1791](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L1791)). Two things postdate it: the Eulerian two-phase solver kept its density frozen until 2026-09-27, so the coupled-droplet studies are an OPEN void decision ([[concepts/eulerian-solver-mass-flux-port]]), and the source family is now subsumed by the gradient-control laws, `sdplsGradientControl` reproducing `R` bit for bit ([[models/sdpls-source]], [[hubs/gradient-control]]).

## Why it matters

The article is the reference for three statements that the gradient-control work inherits: the gradient error is read at T/2 and the shape error at T on a reversed flow ([[concepts/error-vector-and-read-out-instants]]); a source that reads the computed velocity must be verified on the coupled track, because the kinematic ladder cannot see the capillary loop; and the curvature error is the only seed of that loop ([[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]).

## Retracted or superseded inside it

The article keeps its own retraction list ([Negative results, line 2862](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L2862)): "R over-flattens near the resolution limit", "R controls steepening, not flattening" and "beta + explicit diverges at every CFL" were artefacts of the reversed-sign branch; "a flux form cannot drain the droplet", "the coupled Rdiv results are evidence about the loop", "the product-rule residual grows under refinement", "the two remaining Rdiv suspects remain live", "the exponential linearisation changes nothing" and "the exact-curvature Rdiv runs are decisive" were falsified or corrected by later measurements. Two items postdate the article:

1. The Eulerian two-phase solver kept density, mass flux and face viscosity frozen until commit c094bd8 (2026-09-27); with a moving interface and a density contrast that is wrong physics. The coupled-droplet studies of `sec:coupled` ran on that solver. Whether they are void is an OPEN decision ([[concepts/eulerian-solver-mass-flux-port]], [[concepts/wrong-setup-voids]]).
2. Every multi-rank kinematic ladder is provisional until re-run on the fixed binaries; the article says so ([`sec:coupledpatch`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/sdpls-level-set/sdpls-article/sdplsLevelSet.tex#L2678)); the serial re-measurements that reproduce the two load-bearing findings are Tables `tab:rdiv-serial` and `tab:expimplicit`.

The method-comparison article still carries the two retracted SDPLS statements in its limitations ([MC line 208](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L208); see [[studies/method-comparison]]).
