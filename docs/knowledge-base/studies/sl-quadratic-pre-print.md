# The quadratic semi-Lagrangian pre-print and decks

> **Verdict (2026-09-28).** The pre-print `semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex` is the written record of the production advection method: the quadratic value fit at the departure foot, its symbolic curvature, the balanced-force coupling and the static local refinement ([title, line 60](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L60)). It is settled as a document (last commit 768c59e, 2026-09-08). Five statements inside it are superseded by later measurements: the momentum time order ([[decisions/momentum-schemes-bdf2-upwind]]), the blow-up time as a stability proxy ([[retractions/t-blow-baseline]]), the 2D orders quoted in the prose ([[retractions/gradu-coupled-patch-contamination]]), the reading of the late translating instability ([[retractions/late-translating-instability-is-the-outlet]]), and every parallel two-phase number, which predates the seam fixes of 2026-09-27 ([[concepts/coupled-face-density-defect]]). Four SL-baseline sections of the gradient-control pre-print are planned to move into this article on 2026-09-28 ([[studies/gcls-pre-print]]; see the Log).

## Why it matters

The article states the production settings that `METHOD.md` section 8.1 traces: the uncached quadratic value fit ([[decisions/sl-reconstruction-uncached-qwls]]), the point stencil on hexahedra and the face stencil on polyhedra, the pivot tolerance 0.3, the Detrixhe-Aslam indicator ([[decisions/phase-indicator-detrixhe-aslam]]), the reconstructed curvature ([[decisions/surface-tension-reconstructed-curvature]]), the `alg_lin` face viscosity ([[decisions/viscosity-face-model-alg-lin]]) and the rhoLENT mass flux ([[decisions/mass-flux-rholent]]). Its stationary and translating sections carry the measurements behind the source-and-amplifier model of the parasitic current ([[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]).

`src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/` (the `slReconstruction`, `slScheme`, `slValueBound` families), `applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangeLevelSetFoam` (kinematic) and `applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam` (two-phase), `workflow/scripts/leiaRefineHexMesh.py` and `leiaRefinePolyMesh.py` (static refinement), `workflow/Snakefile.sl-quadratic`.

## Retracted or superseded inside it

1. The stabilisation study states that the second-order `backward` momentum scheme destabilises the N=64 droplet and that the L-stable Euler damping is load-bearing ([line 1754](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1754)). The matched-window measurement of 2026-08-20 moved the growth rate by +11.1, +2.9 and -3.0 % (noise), and BDF2 is the production default ([[decisions/momentum-schemes-bdf2-upwind]], [[concepts/ddt-scheme-pairing-bdf2]]). The negative-results deck slide `#/1` carries the same superseded claim.
2. The blow-up times of `sec:droplet` are no longer a stability score ([[retractions/t-blow-baseline]]).
3. The 2D orders in the prose (2.97, 2.59) predate the gradU coupled-patch fix; `uncachedConv2Dvortex` was re-run on 2026-08-27 (table: 2.843, 2.378; the 2.378 is CORRECTED on 2026-10-01, [[retractions/sl-stale-band-alpha-metrics]]). The article was last edited 2026-09-08, so the 3D rows of the same table come from the studies `uncachedConv3Dshear`, `uncachedConv3DshearPoly` and `uncachedConv3DdeformationPoly` that the post-mortem lists as affected; whether they were re-run is not recorded in the sources of this note ([[retractions/gradu-coupled-patch-contamination]]).
4. The translating section was re-run after the closed-box repair, as its own footnote states ([[retractions/closed-box-translating-droplet]]). Its statement that the ratio-838.8 arms destabilise late (8427 to 9987 steps) is now read as an outlet-triggered event with a slower interior growth ([[retractions/late-translating-instability-is-the-outlet]], [[cases/translating-droplet]]).
5. Every parallel two-phase number, including the one-against-four-rank table of `sec:staticrefine-verif` and the N=128 four-rank flux-residual gate, predates the face-density and diagnostics fixes of 2026-09-27 ([[concepts/coupled-face-density-defect]], [[concepts/seam-checks-and-decomposition-invariance]]).
6. The polyhedral Popinet 3D ladder is not in the article; that record is void ([[retractions/polyhedral-popinet-3d-mesh-defect]]). The pivot census (`tab:pivot`) lists a polyhedral Popinet N=64 mesh; whether it is the voided mesh is not recorded.
7. The variational force of `sec:variational` is a proposal with no implementation ([[concepts/variational-capillary-force]]).
