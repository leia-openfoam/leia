# The capillary level-set research roadmap (gates 0-5, 2026-07/08)

> **Verdict (2026-09-28).** `docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md` (1414 lines, 2026-07-30 to 08-07, last commit b7e947e) is the July record: it fixed the target method (cell-centred transport on polyhedra, a geometric indicator, one geometric face mass flux, a pressure-compatible force, [Objective, line 3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L3)), the gate ladder 0 to 5 with gates 4 and 5 open ([Gate status, line 110](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L110)), and the rhoLENT auxiliary density as the reference coupling ([Gate 2 decision, line 76](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L76)). Its dated records established three facts that stand: spatial curvature variation on the force support, not mean bias, produces the parasitic velocity ([2026-07-27, line 724](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L724)); the stabilised foot-point face delivery is second order and the Gaussian term is load-bearing in 3D ([2026-08-06, line 1297](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L1297); [2026-08-07, line 1362](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L1362)); and on perturbed meshes the pressure path itself leaves a residual that neither exact curvature nor analytic geometry removes ([2026-07-28, lines 1028-1147](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L1028-L1147)). Two of its July rules are superseded: `steadyUpwind` as the capillary-case default ([line 525](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L525-L527)) and the structured integral force as a candidate ([[concepts/integral-surface-tension-cst]]). Its P0 item to exchange the processor-patch face fraction ([line 510](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L504-L512)) named the face-density defect that was fixed on 2026-09-27 ([[concepts/coupled-face-density-defect]]).

## Why it matters

The roadmap is where the exact-curvature control became the oracle of every later gate ([[concepts/well-balanced-exact-curvature-gate]]), where the pressure programme was run to its floor on perturbed meshes ([[concepts/pressure-projection-and-linear-solvers]]), and where the second-order face delivery and its 3D Gaussian term were established ([[concepts/face-curvature-deliveries]], [[decisions/curvature-inverse-gaussian]]). Gates 4 and 5 are still open; the September gates are their successors ([[concepts/method-gates]]).

## Decisions

Gate 2: rhoLENT as the reference density coupling, `geometricFaceDensity` as an ablation only ([P0, line 504](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L504)) ([[decisions/mass-flux-rholent]]). P1: do not promote `normalProjection` ([line 513](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L513)) ([[studies/npsl-design]]). P2: no every-step redistancing ([line 536](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L536)) ([[models/redistancer]]). P3: the capillary operator must be derived as a scalar face flux that cancels the rAUf-weighted pressure flux before `fvc::reconstruct`, never as a cell force ([line 552](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L552)) ([[concepts/balanced-force-csf-flux]]). Eight non-orthogonal correctors on perturbed meshes, enforced in the cases ([line 1059](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L1059)). The Gaussian-curvature inverse ([[decisions/curvature-inverse-gaussian]]).

## Retracted or superseded inside it

1. "`steadyUpwind` remains the materialised capillary-case default" ([line 525](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L525-L527)): the production trace is `projectedFlux` with no extension ([[decisions/sl-trace-velocity-projected-flux]]); velocity extension for pure advection is dead end 10 of the curvature plan ([[studies/velocity-extension-pre-print]]).
2. The structured integral force and the conormal prototypes are closed lines ([[concepts/integral-surface-tension-cst]]); the connected-interface family is retired ([[models/curvature-extension]]).
3. The evidence-hierarchy sentence on `quadraticTaylor` (formerly `nestedLSQ`) conflicts with the linear pre-print's description of the same model; OPEN ([[studies/sl-linear-pre-print]]).
4. The July translating-droplet cases (`transIST*`) are separate cases from `translatingDroplet2D`; the closed-box void of 2026-09-02 concerns the latter ([[retractions/closed-box-translating-droplet]]). Whether the `transIST*` meshes carry the inlet and outlet patches their fields declare is not recorded in the sources of this note.
5. The P0 item "replace the processor-patch owner-only face fraction with an exchanged, single-valued coupled-face construction" ([line 510](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L504-L512)) stayed open until 28d13f0 (2026-09-27); every parallel two-phase result between is affected ([[concepts/coupled-face-density-defect]]).
6. The manufactured-pressure-initialisation gate declared on 07-28 was found null on 07-30 ([line 1151](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L1151)).
