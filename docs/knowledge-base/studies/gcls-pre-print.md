# The gradient-controlled level set pre-print (2026-09-27) and its archive

> **Verdict (2026-09-28).** The pre-print `gclsLevelSet.tex` (988 lines, written 2026-09-26/27, commit 8867581) reports the first 2D method-gate campaign of gradient control: six candidates against the production baseline, five arms, three resolutions each, four MPI ranks, a serial check of every parallel result, and every candidate fails ([abstract, line 41](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L42-L68); [`sec:results`, line 614](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L614)). Every number comes from one archive folder named after the library stamp of its runs ([[concepts/data-archive-per-version]]). The status is open: on 2026-09-28 the discussion's reading of the HL1q and HL1z failure as a coupled loop through the velocity is corrected ([[retractions/gcls-coupled-loop-reading]]), two blind spots of the gate scoring are found ([[concepts/method-gates]]), four SL-baseline sections are planned to move to the SL pre-print and a technical report is added (Log).

## Why it matters

The article is the first campaign of the gradient-control line and the first use of the method gate ([[concepts/method-gates]]). Its serial check found two parallel defects of the production coupled solver ([[concepts/coupled-face-density-defect]]), its long-time study settled that the outlet triggers the late translating instability and that a slower interior growth remains ([[retractions/late-translating-instability-is-the-outlet]]), and its baseline oscillating arm showed the gradient drift that gradient control exists to remove ([[cases/oscillating-droplet]]).

## Retracted or superseded inside it

1. **The coupled-loop reading (corrected 2026-09-28).** The discussion explains the HL1q and HL1z destruction of the stationary droplet through a loop from psi to curvature, force, velocity, strain and back at the rate mu ([`sec:discussion`, lines 910-919](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L910-L919)). The linear laws read no velocity (strain weight `none` gives w = 0 and the linear laws have no sigma term), the measured growth rates 245/s and 276/s sit at mu = 270/s, and the crossing-shift formula is first order in h and cannot produce the O(1) shape errors. The primary mechanism is now read as a linear instability of the explicit exponential source with a centred gradient, growing by (1 + mu dt) per step (DERIVED; discriminator: a zero-flow static test). No data is void ([[retractions/gcls-coupled-loop-reading]], [[concepts/why-the-candidates-failed]]).
2. **Two blind spots of the gate scoring (2026-09-28).** The exact-1D closed form is `None` for the candidates in `make_gate_summary.py`, and the band metric is centred; both are repairs on the gate, not on the data ([[concepts/method-gates]]).
3. **The translating horizon.** The gate's 0.05 s horizon was set after the baseline diverged at the pre-registered 0.1 s; the article states it as a decision ([`sec:gate-arms`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L484)); the record of that change is STATUS 11.13 ([lines 3600-3622](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L3600-L3622)).
4. **The 3D gate** has not run; the 3D translating case had no `dropletReferenceVelocity` until 2026-09-27 ([STATUS 11.16, lines 3700-3720](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L3700-L3720)).

## What it does not cover

The Eulerian line as a gate candidate (its curvature pipeline and droplet CSV do not match), 3D, polyhedral meshes, the SDPLS source family ([[studies/sdpls-pre-print]]), the psi filter, and the mechanism of the gradient drift of the oscillating baseline.

## Open questions

The experiments the discussion names: evaluate F on the interface and extend it constant along the normals; replace the band-edge switch by a taper; trace with the extension's own cell velocity; double the radius to R=2h; HL0 on the oscillating droplet at density ratio one ([`sec:discussion`, 846](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L846)); a zero-flow static test of the exponential source ([[retractions/gcls-coupled-loop-reading]]); a longer translating box token and an oscillating horizon ([[concepts/gradient-control-open-decisions]], [[concepts/gradient-control-next-experiments]]).
