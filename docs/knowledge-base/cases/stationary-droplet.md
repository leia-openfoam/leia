# The stationary droplet (2D and 3D)

> Verdict (2026-09-29). A water circle (2D) or sphere (3D) of radius R = 1 mm rests in air without gravity. The exact solution is `u = 0` with the jump `sigma/R = 72.74` Pa in 2D ([SL article `sec:droplet`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1829-L1841)) and `2 sigma/R = 145.48` Pa in 3D ([SL article `sec:staticrefine-verif`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1556-L1561)). Every velocity is a parasitic current, so the case measures the source of that current: the curvature estimator ([[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]). The fixed 2D gate ran on commit 1150e68, 4 ranks, N = 100 / 142 / 200 and T = 0.1 s. There the shape, band-gradient, pressure-jump and curvature errors converge at orders 2.82, 2.08, 2.02 and 1.94. The L2 parasitic current grows from `3.95e-8` to `4.13e-7` m/s, order -3.38 ([`methodGate2D_baseline_orders.csv`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/data/tables/methodGate2D_baseline_orders.csv#L14-L22)). The 6R box reproduced the volume and shape errors of the 10R reference box to 0.16 percent at two cell sizes, and the 4R box failed ([STATUS 4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L749-L767)). That control ran with the psi filter on 8 ranks before the seam fixes, and the record does not re-run it ([`config/domainSizeControl6R.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/domainSizeControl6R.yaml#L54-L72), [STATUS 11.14](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L3686-L3690)).

## What it is

**Geometry.** The 2D mesh is the square `[0, L]^2`, one cell thick, with the four sides in one `walls` patch and empty front and back patches ([`blockMeshDict.template`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet2D/system/blockMeshDict.template#L40-L89)). The walls are no-slip for `U` and zero-gradient for `p_rgh` ([`0.org/U`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet2D/0.org/U#L21-L32)). The 3D mesh is the cube of side L with its six faces in one `walls` patch ([`blockMeshDict.template`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet3D/system/blockMeshDict.template#L33-L74)). The box side is the token `DOMAIN_LENGTH`, default 0.01 m (10R); the droplet is at the centre of the box ([`stationaryDroplet2D.parameter`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet2D.parameter#L18-L35)). The level set starts as the signed distance to the circle, and the phase indicator is `detrixheAslam` ([`fvSolution.template`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet2D/system/fvSolution.template#L393-L407)).

**Fluids.** Water: `rho1 = 998.2` kg/m^3, `nu1 = 1e-6` m^2/s. Air: `rho2 = 1.19` kg/m^3, `nu2 = 1.53e-5` m^2/s. `sigma = 0.07274` N/m ([`cases/default.parameter`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L957-L978)). The density ratio is 838.8. The record gives no Laplace number for this case. With the recorded fluids, `La = sigma rho1 D / mu1^2 = 1.46e5` for `D = 2R` (DERIVED). The capillary time is `T_REF = sqrt(rho R^3/sigma) = 3.7e-3` s ([`methodGate2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/gates/methodGate2D.yaml#L114-L125)).

**Time step.** The step is fixed, `dt = 10.861 h^1.5` s, which is 0.2323 of the capillary limit `sqrt((rho1 + rho2) h^3/(2 pi sigma))` ([`cases/default.parameter`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L967-L973), [`controlDict.template`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet2D/system/controlDict.template#L66-L74)). The step follows the cell size h, not the cell count, so a smaller box at equal h gets the same step ([STATUS 4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L781-L787), [[concepts/capillary-time-step]]).

**The metrics.** The solver writes one CSV row per step ([`createDropletMetricsFile.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/createDropletMetricsFile.H#L1-L44)). The gate reads six of its columns ([`make_gate_summary.py`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/workflow/scripts/make_gate_summary.py#L206-L233)):

| gate quantity | CSV column and formula |
|---|---|
| shape error | `zeroSetRadialL2 / R`: the L2 radial distance of the psi = 0 crossings to the exact circle |
| volume error | `phaseVolumeRelError`: the relative change of the liquid volume from t = 0 |
| band gradient error | `gradPsiL2ErrorBand`: the L2 norm of `q - 1` in the band, with `q` the magnitude of `grad psi` (unlimited gradient) |
| spurious current | `l2MagUPrime` (L2) and `meanMagUPrime` (L1) of `U - U_ref` |
| pressure-jump error | `abs(pLaplace - sigma kappa)/(sigma kappa)`, pure-phase means with `eps = 1e-2` |
| curvature error | `kErrL2Band R/(dims - 1)`: the L2 error of kappa in the band, relative to the exact kappa |

The solver also writes L_inf columns (`maxMagU`, `zeroSetRadialLinf`, `kErrLinfBand`). They carry no verdict, because a single-cell extremum does not converge ([`writeDropletMetrics.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/writeDropletMetrics.H#L14-L19)). The pressure jump uses the pure phases, `alpha > 0.99` and `alpha < 0.01`, not the `alpha = 1/2` partition ([`writeDropletMetrics.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/writeDropletMetrics.H#L185-L221)).

**The ladders.** The record holds five ladders of this case:

1. The SL article ladder, 2D, 10R box, N = 32 to 256; N = 128 and 256 diverged ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1843-L1865)).
2. The 2D shared ladders, 10R box, N = 32 / 64 / 128 / 256, 4 ranks, `none` against `cellCentreInverse` ([`stationaryLadder2Dshared.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/stationaryLadder2Dshared.yaml#L40-L60)).
3. The domain-size control, 2D, boxes 10R, 6R and 4R at h = 1e-4 and 5e-5 ([STATUS 4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L732-L787)).
4. The 3D production-force ladder on the 6R box, N_fine = 60 / 76 / 96 / 120 (R/h = 10 to 20), uniform against statically refined, t = 0.025 s ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1628-L1662)).
5. The gate arm, 2D, 10R box, N = 100 / 142 / 200 (R/h = 10 / 14.2 / 20), 9207 / 15580 / 26042 steps ([`methodGate2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/gates/methodGate2D.yaml#L114-L125), [`methodGate2D_baseline_summary.csv`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/data/tables/methodGate2D_baseline_summary.csv#L9-L11)). The 3D gate arm (6R box, N = 60 / 78 / 102, T = 0.025 s) exists but never ran, because no candidate passed in 2D ([`methodGate3D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/gates/methodGate3D.yaml#L85-L96), [STATUS 11.15](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L4040)).

**The exact-curvature arms.** The force model `constantCurvatureSurfaceTension` with `CONST_CURVATURE` = 1/R or 2/R makes the force an exact discrete gradient. The template calls it a mechanism diagnostic, not a production model ([`fvSolution.template`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet2D/system/fvSolution.template#L248-L269)). On this case it ran as the 3D hanging-node gate, `stationaryDroplet3D{refined,uniform}WB` with kappa = 2000 1/m ([`stationaryDroplet3DuniformWB.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/stationaryDroplet3DuniformWB.yaml#L5-L19)). The 2D kick and amplifier gates ran on the translating case with a `U0 = 0` arm ([`kickOriginGate2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/kickOriginGate2D.yaml#L46-L62)). All of them are in [[concepts/well-balanced-exact-curvature-gate]].

## Why it matters

The exact velocity is zero, so every velocity in the CSV is error, and no reference solution is necessary. The campaign found the source of the parasitic current on this case. An exact curvature leaves the velocity at the solver's round-off floor, so the current of the reconstructed curvature is estimator error ([[concepts/well-balanced-exact-curvature-gate]], [[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]). Two production settings rest on this case alone: `MASS_FLUX rhoLENT` and `CURVATURE_EXTENSION cellCentreInverse` ([METHOD 8.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L389-L393), [[decisions/mass-flux-rholent]], [[decisions/curvature-extension-cell-centre-inverse]]). The 3D twin is necessary because the 3D inverse uses the Gaussian curvature K, which is zero in 2D ([`stationaryDroplet3D.parameter`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/stationaryDroplet3D.parameter#L4-L18)). The case cannot test transport: the interface moves by round-off only, so a defect that scales with the displacement stays invisible here ([[cases/translating-droplet]], [[cases/oscillating-droplet]]).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

The case itself is sound; the method fails on it in two ways. First, the parasitic current does not converge at a fixed horizon. The gate order is -3.38, and the SL article ladder diverged at N = 128 and 256 ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1856-L1865)). The two-factor law explains the trend: the kick falls with h, but the amplifier `G` grows with resolution ([[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]). The gcls pre-print reads the gate order as a slower approach to equilibrium on the finer mesh. No run separates that reading from growth (HYPOTHESIS, [gcls article `sec:res-baseline`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L574-L577)). Second, the recorded blow-up times of the article ladder (0.47 / 0.44 / 0.11 / 0.03 s) are not a baseline for new runs ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1890-L1896), [[retractions/t-blow-baseline]]).

## Decisions

- The 2D gate runs on the 10R box and the 3D gate on the 6R box, which its header calls certified ([`methodGate2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/gates/methodGate2D.yaml#L114-L125), [`methodGate3D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/gates/methodGate3D.yaml#L6-L10)).
- The spurious current is scored in L1 and L2 of `U - U_ref`, never in L_inf ([gcls article `sec:gate-metrics`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/gradient-controlled-level-set/gcls-level-set-article/gclsLevelSet.tex#L500-L523), [[concepts/error-vector-and-read-out-instants]]).
- The arm runs `cellCentreInverse` and the explicit `twoPhaseCoupling` block of the gate ([`methodGate2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/gates/methodGate2D.yaml#L61-L86)).
