# The static curvature gates: circle, ellipse, ellipsoid

> Verdict (2026-09-29). The static gates score the delivered curvature on an exact interface without flow, on one rank, with one curvature evaluation per resolution ([STATUS 3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L380-L383)). The circle cannot rank the inverse-based deliveries: all of them are second order there, 1.97 to 2.00 ([PCS 14.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1144-L1145)). The 2:1 ellipse, with an exact curvature from 250 to 2000 1/m, decides accuracy. There the per-face inverse keeps order 1.98. Both one-value-per-cut-cell deliveries fall to 1.02 and 1.03 and are 21 times less accurate at N = 512 ([PCS 12](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1004-L1018)). That result retracted the cell-mean delivery ([[retractions/cell-mean-delivery-adoption]]). On the sphere the Gaussian-curvature-aware inverse of the per-face delivery converges at `h^1.95`, against `h^1.02` without K ([RM](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L1378-L1388)). These sphere orders do not belong to the cell-centre inverse ([METHOD 4.3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/METHOD.md#L266-L269)). A new delivery passes only with `G h^2 <= 0.65` and an L2 order `>= 1.9` on the ellipse gate, not on the circle ([STATUS 7](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2703-L2706)). The production delivery since 2026-09-01, `cellCentreInverse`, was scored on the ellipse and the ellipsoid for the first time on 2026-09-29 ([STATUS 11.19](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/STATUS.md#L4269-L4279)). On the signed-distance ellipse its error is 3.28 / 0.831 / 0.208 1/m at N = 128 / 256 / 512, at orders 1.98 and 2.00 ([METHOD 4.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/METHOD.md#L175-L177)). On the signed-distance ellipsoid it converges at 2.10 over N = 50 to 128, and at 1.00 without K ([METHOD 4.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/METHOD.md#L178-L183)). Its gain meets the criterion as PCS 12 applied it: 0.647 at N = 512, equal to the per-face inverse ([METHOD 4.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/f47fc939/METHOD.md#L187-L190)). It fails the per-rung form at N = 128 and 256 (0.651, 0.673); at N = 256 every second-order delivery fails that form ([METHOD 4.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/f47fc939/METHOD.md#L190-L193)). Only the first-order cell-mean (0.416) and symmetric face-mean (0.467) deliveries stay below 0.65 at every rung. Among the second-order deliveries the gain therefore does not separate on the ellipse ([METHOD 4.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/f47fc939/METHOD.md#L192-L194)).

The circle gate `curvatureDroplet2D` is a config on the stationary case, not a case folder. `make curvature` runs it through [`workflow/Snakefile.curvature`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/workflow/Snakefile.curvature#L1-L58) and writes `curvature_error.{csv,tex}` and `sl_curvature_error.png` into the SL theme. The ellipse has the semi-axes 2 mm and 1 mm in the 10 mm box, so `kappa(theta) = a b/(a^2 sin^2 theta + b^2 cos^2 theta)^(3/2)`. Its tightest radius of curvature is 0.5 mm, 3.2 cells at N = 64; N = 64 is a trend point only ([`ellipseDroplet2D.parameter`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/ellipseDroplet2D.parameter#L1-L32)). The ellipsoid has the half-axes 0.21, 0.15 and 0.107, with the volume of the sphere gate's R = 0.1503. It runs the signed distance and the implicit function ([`faceCurvatureEllipsoid3D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/faceCurvatureEllipsoid3D.yaml#L1-L36)). Its run of 2026-08-26 was never curated; the re-run of 2026-09-29, which scores `cellCentreInverse`, is the first curated result ([config header](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/config/faceCurvatureEllipsoid3D.yaml#L15-L17)).

**The two numbers.** Every gate reports two numbers per delivery:

1. Accuracy: the `abs(Sf)`-weighted L2 norm of `kappa_f - kappa_exact` on the active faces (`snGrad(alpha)` not zero). The app assembles `kappa_f` exactly as the solver's force path does. The ellipse gate takes the local exact value at each face centre ([`leiaTestMeanCurvature.C`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/test/leiaTestMeanCurvature/leiaTestMeanCurvature.C#L13-L58), [`faceCurvatureEllipse2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/faceCurvatureEllipse2D.yaml#L29-L32)).
2. Gain: `G = norm2(d kappa_f) / normInf(d psi)` for the perturbation `psi + eps h r`, with `r` a pseudo-random field in [-1, 1] and alpha held fixed. The gate reports `G h^2`: 1 means as sensitive as a second difference of psi ([`leiaTestCurvatureNoiseGain.C`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/test/leiaTestCurvatureNoiseGain/leiaTestCurvatureNoiseGain.C#L11-L57)). The gain is constant to about 3 percent over N = 64 to 256 and linear in `eps` over a range of 100 ([PCS 10](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L743-L745)).

**What each gate can see.** A circle or a sphere has a constant curvature and a parallel foliation, so an averaging error and the foliation residual `D` are both zero there ([`faceCurvatureEllipse2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/faceCurvatureEllipse2D.yaml#L3-L16), [PCS 11.2](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L832-L839)). The signed-distance ellipse adds a varying curvature and keeps `D = 0`. The quadratic-form ellipse has an exact fit and `D` not zero, so it measures the inverse's model error alone ([`faceCurvatureEllipsoidPsi2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/faceCurvatureEllipsoidPsi2D.yaml#L5-L19)). The sphere adds the Gaussian curvature K. The weakest hypothesis of the inverse is a parallel foliation, not a signed distance ([PCS 11.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L792-L796), [[concepts/cell-centre-inverse-curvature]]).

## Why it matters

A static gate evaluates the curvature once per resolution, where a coupled run takes thousands of steps. The gates reject a candidate delivery before any coupled run ([STATUS 1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L329-L331)). But accuracy alone ranks deliveries wrongly. Over three deliveries the static L2 error fell `45.8 -> 1.67 -> 1.18` 1/m while the coupled blow-up time fell `0.0803 -> 0.0668 -> 0.0202` s ([PCS 10](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L725-L729)). The gain orders the same three deliveries correctly and does not predict the magnitude of the growth ([PCS 10](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L737-L751), [STATUS 1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L322-L327)). So the criterion asks for both numbers, and on a geometry that is not simpler than the interfaces the method runs on ([PCS 12](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1056-L1062)). The stationary droplet cannot show the averaging defect, by construction ([PCS 12](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1035-L1042), [[cases/stationary-droplet]]).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

Two families of delivery failed the ellipse gate. The cut-cell and the cell-mean deliveries assign one value to every active face of a cut cell. Where the curvature varies along the interface, each face then receives a value centred on the cell, an offset of `O(h dkappa/ds)`. That is first order by construction, and better averaging cannot repair it ([PCS 12](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1020-L1028)). The symmetric face mean is symmetric in geometry but restricted to active faces. The active mask depends on the interface position in the cell, so the mean is offset at `O(h)` too ([PCS 14.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1137-L1142)). Every construction that lowered `G h^2` below the per-face value lost an order on the ellipse. Within face-value averaging, a lower gain always cost an order ([PCS 14.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1147-L1150), [[concepts/face-curvature-deliveries]]).

## Decisions

- The acceptance criterion: `G h^2 <= 0.65` and fitted L2 order `>= 1.9` on the ellipse gate, not on the circle; a candidate that meets only the order is a regression ([STATUS 7](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2703-L2706), [PCS 12](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1044-L1047)). It has two forms: PCS 12 applied it at the finest rung with the fitted order; the pre-registration of 2026-09-29 applies it at every rung N >= 128 ([METHOD 4.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/f47fc939/METHOD.md#L188-L191)).
- The cut-cell family is retired; the cell-mean adoption is retracted ([STATUS 1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L348-L352), [[retractions/cell-mean-delivery-adoption]]).
- `CURVATURE_INVERSE_GAUSSIAN yes`, decided on the sphere gate ([METHOD 8.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L401), [[decisions/curvature-inverse-gaussian]]).
- Before 2026-09-01 the per-face inverse `stabilizedFootPointFace` was production and the only delivery recorded as passing, in the form PCS 12 applied; since c935883 production is `cellCentreInverse` ([STATUS 1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L341-L346), [[decisions/curvature-extension-cell-centre-inverse]]). In that form `cellCentreInverse` passes too, with the same 0.647 at N = 512. In the per-rung form the per-face inverse fails at N = 256 (0.673) and `cellCentreInverse` at N = 128 and 256 ([STATUS 11.19](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/f47fc939/STATUS.md#L4291-L4296)).
- 2026-09-29: no setting changed. `CURVATURE_EXTENSION cellCentreInverse` stays the global default ([METHOD 8.1](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/METHOD.md#L423)). The static result does not decide the coupled translating and oscillating questions ([[decisions/curvature-extension-cell-centre-inverse]]).
