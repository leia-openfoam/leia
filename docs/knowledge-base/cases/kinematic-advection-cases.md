# The kinematic advection cases

> Verdict (2026-09-29). The kinematic cases prescribe the velocity, so no force, pressure or curvature enters the loop and every error is transport error ([2Dtranslation.parameter L1-L3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/2Dtranslation.parameter#L1-L3), [[models/level-set-advection]]). On the 2D vortex the shape-error order of the quadratic semi-Lagrangian scheme is 2.97 at CFL 1/2 and 2.59 at CFL 1. On the 3D shear it is 2.95 on hex and 3.28 on polyhedra, and on the filament-limited 3D deformation 1.36 and 1.46 ([SL article L1371-L1430](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1371-L1430)). The polyhedral orders ran with the clip on, which production no longer uses ([uncachedConv3DshearPoly.yaml L30-L38](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/uncachedConv3DshearPoly.yaml#L30-L38), [[concepts/value-bounds-and-clips]]). The 2D vortex and the 3D deformation have u = 0 on every wall. Such a gate never moves the foot in the wall cells, so it does not test the boundary stencils ([CLAUDE L516-L526](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L516-L526)). Two polyhedral defects of 2026-09-05 hid in exactly those cells ([STATUS L1584-L1593](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1584-L1593), [L1900-L1915](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1900-L1915)). The regression set names `2Dtranslation` as the only O(1)-displacement gate ([CLAUDE L590-L594](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L590-L594), [[concepts/advection-regression-set]]). Its dictionary has no `oscillation` entry. So the base default reverses the translation: the circle travels at most about 0.16 and returns, not 0.5 as its comments state (DERIVED, see Open questions).

## What it is

Each case sets a `velocityModel` in `system/fvSolution`. The solver takes the cell velocity and the face flux from the analytic field. It does so on internal faces and on every non-coupled boundary face ([velocityModel.C L117-L232](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/velocityModel.C#L117-L232), `src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/velocityModel.C`). With `oscillation on`, the solver multiplies both by `cos(pi t / tau)`, with `tau = endTime` by default, so the interface returns to its initial state at `t = T` ([velocityModel.C L49-L50](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/velocityModel.C#L49-L50), [L80-L105](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/velocityModel.C#L80-L105)). The default of that entry is `on` ([velocityModel.C L49](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/velocityModel.C#L49)). The velocity formulas are in [prescribedVelocityModels.C L61-L206](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/prescribedVelocityModels.C#L61-L206).

The error metrics are the same in every case. `E_GEOM_ALPHA = sum_c V_c abs(alpha_c - alpha0_c)`, relative to `sum_c V_c alpha0_c`; `E_VOL_ALPHA_REL` is the relative phase-volume change; `E_BOUND_ALPHA` is the largest over- or undershoot of alpha ([advectionErrors.H L48-L79](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangeLevelSetFoam/advectionErrors.H#L48-L79), [advectionErrorsCsv.H L31-L55](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/advectionErrorsCsv.H#L31-L55)). `alpha0` is the initial field unless the case supplies `alphaEnd`. So the shape error is an error only for a reversed flow ([errorCalculation.H L93-L118](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangeLevelSetFoam/errorCalculation.H#L93-L118), [aggregate.py L316-L329](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/workflow/scripts/aggregate.py#L316-L329)). The gradient error comes from a function object. On a reversed flow read the gradient at T/2, the shape at T and the volume at both ([CLAUDE L507-L514](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L507-L514), [README L151-L158](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/workflow/README.md#L151-L158), [[concepts/error-vector-and-read-out-instants]]).

## Why it matters

The research loop puts kinematic transport before any coupled run, because a coupled failure is otherwise ambiguous between transport and force ([CLAUDE L474-L497](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L474-L497)). `2Dtranslation` exists for that reason. The coupled translating droplet failed while the stationary droplet was clean, and no translating kinematic rung had run ([kinematicTranslation2D.yaml L4-L15](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/kinematicTranslation2D.yaml#L4-L15)). Its result exonerated the transport. The coupled droplet runs at an interface Courant number of 0.0059, more than 40 times below the cheapest arm of the gate ([L70-L75](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/kinematicTranslation2D.yaml#L70-L75)). Every change to the advection path re-runs three of these cases at three resolutions each ([CLAUDE L583-L598](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L583-L598)).

## The boundary-stencil trap

A discretisation error of the form coefficient x displacement^n needs a gate with O(1) displacement in the cells it can affect ([CLAUDE L516-L526](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L516-L526)). On a wall with u = 0 the foot stays on the cell centre, and the error is invisible. The record has three defects of this kind:

1. The quadratic fit on cfMesh's boundary-layer polyhedra. The offending cells have 10 point neighbours for 9 coefficients, condition numbers 3e7 to 6e12 and coefficients 3e2 to 3e8. The fit is exact at zero displacement and wrong by coefficient x (U dt)^2 at the foot ([STATUS L1584-L1593](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1584-L1593)). The first translating polyhedral case put the far field 1.4 h wrong after one step and diverged at step 3 ([CLAUDE L522-L523](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L522-L523)). The fix is the pivot tolerance `SL_QUAD_PIVOT_TOL 0.3` ([DP L1060-L1081](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L1060-L1081), [[models/sl-reconstruction]]).
2. Boundary faces as stencil data. With `psi zeroGradient`, each boundary face gives the cell's own value at h/2 with a large weight, and the fit's normal gradient collapses. The level set then moves normal to an inflow or outflow boundary at only 8 % to 49 % of the exact rate ([DP L1083-L1100](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L1083-L1100), [STATUS L1900-L1915](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1900-L1915)).
3. The far-field checkerboard in the small one-sided outlet cells. The sigma = 0 coupled control in a uniform stream is the missing kinematic gate, and it fails at step 508 ([STATUS L1840-L1852](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1840-L1852), [[concepts/polyhedral-fit-amplification]]).

The velocity on the walls of each case is in the table above. Only the translations, the rotation, the 3D shear on its top and bottom walls and the contact-line cases move the wall cells.

## Decisions

- The standing advection regression set has three rungs: `2Dvortex` or `2Dtranslation` on hex, `3Dshear` on hex and `3Dshear` on pMesh. Each runs at three resolutions, and an inert change must be byte-identical there ([CLAUDE L583-L598](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L583-L598), [[concepts/advection-regression-set]]).
- No advection result from one resolution; report the order next to the error ([CLAUDE L554-L581](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L554-L581), [[concepts/richardson-ladders-and-orders]]).
- The curated tables blank the shape error of a non-reversed flow, because it then measures the travel, not an error ([aggregate.py L316-L329](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/workflow/scripts/aggregate.py#L316-L329)).
- Production runs on the hexahedral family; the polyhedral rung measures the amplification defect ([[decisions/mesh-family-hexahedral]]).

## Open questions

1. `2Dtranslation` runs a reversed translation, not the one-way translation its comments describe. DERIVED from three facts:
   1. the `velocityModel` block sets no `oscillation` entry ([fvSolution.template L110-L120](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/2Dtranslation/system/fvSolution.template#L110-L120)), and the base default is `on` ([velocityModel.C L49-L50](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/velocityModel.C#L49-L50));
   2. the solver applies the factor to every model ([leiaSemiLagrangeLevelSetFoam.C L136-L150](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangeLevelSetFoam/leiaSemiLagrangeLevelSetFoam.C#L136-L150));
   3. no `alphaEnd` exists, so a one-way translation would give `E_GEOM_ALPHA_REL` near 2, not the recorded 4.747e-03 to 7.947e-05 ([METHOD L645-L648](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L645-L648)).

   With tau = 0.5 the circle reaches x = 0.25 + 0.5/pi, about 0.41, and returns. The comment says it travels to x = 0.75 ([2Dtranslation.parameter L28-L29](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/2Dtranslation.parameter#L28-L29)). The wall cells still move at every step, so the gate still tests the boundary stencils. The `deltaT` sequence in the solver log discriminates the two readings: under the reversal the time step follows `abs(cos)`, under a steady translation it stays constant after the start. Under this reading the case text and its exact-solution statement are wrong. Whether the rule for a wrong setup voids the data is an author decision ([CLAUDE L705-L716](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L705-L716), [[concepts/wrong-setup-voids]]).
2. `3Dtranslation` is the 3D translation through an inlet and an outlet, and no study config runs it. The record ran that test only as the coupled sigma = 0 control ([STATUS L1846-L1848](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1846-L1848)). Its polyhedral parameter file sets `END_TIME 3.0` ([3Dtranslation_poly.parameter L6](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/3Dtranslation_poly.parameter#L6)). At U = 1 the sphere then leaves the [0, 2] box at t = 1.65 (DERIVED).
3. No converged polyhedral ladder with the production default is on record. The published ladders ran with the clip, and the default diverges at step 198 on the coarsest rung. The 3D rungs of the 2026-09-10 ladders were not reported ([METHOD L679-L680](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L679-L680)).
4. The 3D shear field has w not zero on the top and bottom walls (DERIVED from [prescribedVelocityModels.C L97-L108](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/velocityModel/prescribedVelocityModels.C#L97-L108)). Whether the rung tests the boundary stencils of those walls is not measured.
