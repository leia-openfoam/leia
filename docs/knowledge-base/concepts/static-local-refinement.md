# Static local refinement around the interface

> Verdict (2026-09-28). The method reads and writes the interface only in a narrow band, so a band around the initial interface is refined once, before the run, and the far field stays coarse; the solver and its level-set dictionary are not changed ([SL article `sec:staticrefine` L985-L998](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L985-L998)). Four complete fine layers on each side are the stencil minimum; the default requests six ([SL article L1010-L1011](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1010-L1011), [README L448-L453](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L448-L453)). Two gates decided it on the 3D stationary droplet. The constant-curvature well-balanced gate gives the Laplace jump 145.470 Pa on refined and uniform meshes with the spurious velocity at the solver's round-off floor, so hanging nodes do not break the balanced force ([STATUS L1207-L1226](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1207-L1226)). The production-force ladder at N_fine = 60, 76, 96, 120 meets every pre-registered tolerance, with fitted orders never more than 0.09 apart (shape 2.08 on both meshes) at 3.4 to 6.9 times fewer core-hours, 13.4 times with two levels ([STATUS L1266-L1292](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1266-L1292), [refined_convergence_orders.tex](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/data/tables/refined_convergence_orders.tex)). The polyhedral twin is equivalent too, at 2.7 times fewer core-hours ([STATUS L1298-L1307](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1298-L1307)).

## What it is

Two pre-processing drivers, called from the workflow's `mesh` rule with `mesh: hexRefined | polyRefined` ([README L413-L437](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L413-L437)):

1. `leiaRefineHexMesh.py`: `blockMesh`, then `REFINE_LEVELS` passes of: reset `0/` from `0.org`, `leiaSetFields` on the current mesh, `topoSet` with the seed `0 < alpha < 1` (the faces where `snGrad(alpha)` is non-zero, the force support) dilated through faces, `refineHexMesh` (hexRef8, 2x2x2, cell and point levels persisted so that the 2:1 constraint holds across passes) ([leiaRefineHexMesh.py L9-L25](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/scripts/leiaRefineHexMesh.py#L9-L25), [SL article L1038-L1051](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1038-L1051)).
2. `leiaRefinePolyMesh.py`: cfMesh has no in-place refiner, so the polyhedral analogue writes the `psi = 0` iso-surface as an STL and re-meshes with `surfaceMeshRefinement { additionalRefinementLevels i; refinementThickness bandCells h_i }` ([leiaRefinePolyMesh.py L2-L25](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/scripts/leiaRefinePolyMesh.py#L2-L25), [SL article L1075-L1088](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1075-L1088)).

Both end with `0/ := 0.org; leiaSetFields` on the final mesh: nothing mapped through a split survives, because a mapped alpha is a smeared alpha ([README L439-L441](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L439-L441), [SL article L1052-L1057](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1052-L1057)). The band check fails the mesh rule (exit 2) when an interface cell lies outside the fine region, when fewer than four complete fine layers remain at the worst point, or when `N_CELLS`, the capillary-dt handle, does not encode the fine spacing ([README L441-L446](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L441-L446)). The number of dilations is measured, not derived: face dilation grows a Manhattan diamond and buys only about 1.15 fine cells per dilation along the diagonals of a sphere, so 3, 4, 5 dilations leave 4.6, 5.8, 7.0 complete fine layers at R/h = 10 ([SL article L1061-L1066](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1061-L1066)). The driver adds dilations until the signed distance on the current mesh proves the width, `2 abs(psi_c)/h_c - 1 >= n_band`, for every unselected cell ([L1066-L1069](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1066-L1069)).

The reach of one time step from the interface, in cell layers, is the design basis ([SL article table `tab:layers` L1018-L1036](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1018-L1036)): phase indicator 1, capillary force support and `fvc::reconstruct` 2, `kappa_f` from the cell-point-cell fit 3, one semi-Lagrangian step of a layer-3 value 4, the explicit viscous transpose term 4. Tokens: `REFINE_LEVELS 0` (inert default), `REFINE_BAND_CELLS 6`, `REFINE_SOURCE interface | ball`; derived `N_CELLS_BASE = N_CELLS / 2^REFINE_LEVELS` ([README L448-L455](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L448-L455)).

## Why it matters

A uniform mesh pays for the interface resolution everywhere: the droplet occupies 0.1 % of the 6R box, R/h = 10 already costs 216 000 hexahedra, the polyhedral rungs 572 000 to 3.6e6 cells, and R/h = 40 would need 1.4e7 ([SL article L987-L991](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L987-L991)). The capillary step is set by the fine spacing, so a refined mesh and its uniform twin take the same step and the same number of steps; only the cell count differs, and the ratio grows with resolution because the band is a surface while the box is a volume ([L1090-L1095](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1090-L1095)). The objection was precise: the potential-form identity `kappa grad alpha = grad(kappa alpha) - alpha grad kappa` holds at a face only with the central weight 1/2, so a 2:1 transition face could inject a spurious current no estimator is responsible for ([SL article L1379-L1384](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1379-L1384), [[concepts/balanced-force-csf-flux]]). The transitions lie outside the force support by construction, and the gate settled the question by measurement ([L1011-L1016](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L1011-L1016)).

## Decisions

- Hanging nodes do not break the balanced force; the octree rejection of `stationaryDroplet3Dwide` is retracted in full, its hanging-node argument by the constant-curvature gate and its equal-cost argument by 3.4 to 13.4x ([STATUS L1223-L1226](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1223-L1226), [L1290-L1292](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1290-L1292)).
- Static refinement is equivalent to the uniform mesh at matched fine spacing ([STATUS L1260-L1292](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1260-L1292)); `REFINE_BAND_CELLS 6` is the margin over the stencil minimum of four ([STATUS L1316-L1317](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1316-L1317)).
- Two pass lines were mis-set on the same day for the same reason: round-off-level lines for quantities the linear solver limits at its tolerance ([STATUS L1244-L1246](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1244-L1246), [[concepts/pressure-projection-and-linear-solvers]]).

## Open questions

1. The refinement is static: a moving interface leaves its band. Adaptivity needs the stencils and the semi-Lagrangian caches rebuilt after every topology change, and the re-initialisation is not available mid-run ([SL article L2498-L2505](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2498-L2505), [STATUS L2308-L2310](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2308-L2310)).
2. The decomposition differences of the ladder were measured on four ranks before the coupled-face density fix of 2026-09-27, so the attribution to the linear solver is not yet separated from that seam error ([METHOD L304-L312](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L304-L312), [[concepts/coupled-face-density-defect]]).
3. The polyhedral saving is limited by the mesher's grading (11.8 layers for 6) and by the boundary refinement of its "uniform" twin ([STATUS L1303-L1305](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1303-L1305)).
