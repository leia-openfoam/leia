# Clips and value bounds: what was falsified

> Verdict (2026-09-29, record to 2026-09-28). No bound on the reconstructed value is in the best configuration ([METHOD 8.1 L380-L383](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L380-L383)). Two value bounds were measured from 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-10, and both are falsified as a fix. The quasi-monotone clip removes the polyhedral far-field failure ([STATUS L2035-L2039](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2035-L2039)). On the 2D hexahedral translating droplet at N = 64 it costs +30.4 % volume error ([STATUS L2421-L2425](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2421-L2425)). The narrow band is not the cause. Every firing is in one of the six cells where psi is its own stencil extremum ([STATUS L2429-L2433](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2429-L2433), [[retractions/clip-damage-is-the-narrow-band]]). The exemption of those cells cuts the cost to +1.03 % ([STATUS L2449-L2456](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2449-L2456)). Gate G4 then measures the polyhedral failure at step 506, against step 527 without a clip ([STATUS L2337-L2342](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2337-L2342)). The distance-cone bound improved every coupled interface metric by 25 to 68 % at N = 64 ([METHOD 8.3.2 L539-L542](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L539-L542)). The advection ladders and a second resolution falsified it on the same day, 2026-09-10 ([METHOD 8.3 L490-L498](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L490-L498), [[retractions/distance-cone-bound-as-transport-bound]]). The method comparison measured hard slope limiters on the reversed vortex before this campaign (article text of 2df993c, 2026-07-30). They collapse the shape-error order: Barth-Jespersen 3.0 to 0.1, Venkatakrishnan 3.0 to 0.9 ([MC article L264-L266](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L264-L266)). A nonnegative, partition-of-unity, quadratic-exact rule cannot exist at an off-node point, so the quadratic fit must have negative weights ([METHOD 9 L786-L789](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L786-L789)). The polyhedral rung of the regression set adds a diagnostic. A mesh change of about 3e-12 relative moves the volume error of the cone bound by 14.2 %. It moves the volume error of the other two arms by 0.0 % ([CLAUDE L606-L630](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L606-L630)).

## What it is

A value bound acts after the fit. The quadratic weighted-least-squares fit gives the departure value `H_c = psi^n(x_d)` of the arrival cell c ([[models/sl-reconstruction]]). The bound replaces it by `min(max(H_c, lo_c), hi_c)`, with one interval `[lo_c, hi_c]` per cell ([slCorrector.C L93-L140](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/slCorrector.C#L93-L140), `src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/slCorrector.C`). The bound never changes the fit, the foot or the field. It acts in `slCorrector::robustEvaluate`, so only the `pointValue` scheme sees it ([slValueBound.H L30-L67](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/slValueBound.H#L30-L67), `src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/slValueBound.H`). The members and their keys are in [[models/sl-value-bound]]; this note records what each bound did.

| bound | interval, or what it limits | dictionary word, token | purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| no bound | runaway cap only: stencil mid +- 10 stencil ranges | `none` (production) | the control |
| quasi-monotone clip | `lo_c = min_j psi_j`, `hi_c = max_j psi_j` over the stencil | `stencilBounds`, legacy `clipToStencilBounds`, `SL_CLIP` | stop the polyhedral far-field growth |
| clip keys | `clipRegion all` or `outsideBand`; `clipKeepExtrema` exempts a cell with `psi_c = lo_c` or `psi_c = hi_c` | `SL_CLIP_REGION`, `SL_CLIP_KEEP_EXTREMA` | spare the interface |
| distance-cone bound | `lo_c = max_j (psi_j - L abs(x_d - x_j))`, `hi_c = min_j (psi_j + L abs(x_d - x_j))`, L = 1 | `lipschitzCone`, `SL_VALUE_BOUND` | admit a medial-axis extremum |
| slope limiter | the slope of the fit, not the value | `SL_LIMITER` (`barthJespersen`, `venkatakrishnan`) | boundedness |

Sources of the table: [DP L57-L160](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L57-L160), [DP L260-L262](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L260-L262), [METHOD 8.3.1 L502-L510](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L502-L510).

The reason for a bound is the Lebesgue constant of the fit, `Lambda_c = abs(1 - sum_j g_j) + sum_j abs(g_j)`, with `g_j` the weight of the stencil value `psi_j` in `H_c`. `Lambda_c` bounds what one step can do to any mode ([STATUS L2160-L2164](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2160-L2164)). A reconstruction with `Lambda_c = 1` in every cell cannot create or grow a new extremum. The clip enforces that property ([STATUS L2195-L2201](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2195-L2201), [[concepts/polyhedral-fit-amplification]]).

## Why it matters

The polyhedral far field fails without a bound. The sigma = 0 control on the Popinet 3D pMesh keeps an exact uniform stream (max abs u' 2.3e-14). The passive level set still grows a fake zero set at step 508 ([STATUS L1840-L1846](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1840-L1846), [[cases/popinet-translating-droplet]]). The 3D polyhedral kinematic gates passed only because u = 0 on their walls ([STATUS L1847-L1849](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1847-L1849), [[cases/kinematic-advection-cases]]). `Lambda_c` reaches 1.2608 in the small one-sided pMesh cells, against 1.0527 on hexahedra ([STATUS L2166-L2179](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2166-L2179)). A smaller time step cannot help: the accumulated bound `exp((Lambda - 1) T/dt) = exp(c U T)` does not contain dt ([STATUS L2181-L2190](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2181-L2190)). So the fix must change the update operator ([popinet2D_clipRegionGate.yaml L3-L9](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/popinet2D_clipRegionGate.yaml#L3-L9), `config/popinet2D_clipRegionGate.yaml`). The classical candidate is a clip of the reconstructed value to the stencil bounds ([STATUS L1852-L1854](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L1852-L1854)).

A bound must also be inert where nothing is broken. The 2D hexahedral translating droplet completes its 1563 steps without a bound, so it measures the cost of a bound ([popinet2D_coneBoundGate.yaml L13-L19](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/popinet2D_coneBoundGate.yaml#L13-L19), `config/popinet2D_coneBoundGate.yaml`). No formulation on record meets both requirements ([STATUS L2394-L2399](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2394-L2399)). The same missing maximum principle makes Eikonal redistancing unsafe. A redistancer rebuilds distance to every zero set it gets, advection noise included ([METHOD 9 L790-L794](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L790-L794), [[concepts/redistancing-geometric-grl]]).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

1. The clip. A monotone bound cannot represent an extremum. Where `psi_c` is the stencil minimum, `lo_c = psi_c`, and the clip pulls every lower value back up. The fit undershoots at the cone apex, because a smooth quadratic cannot follow a non-differentiable minimum. So the clip flattens the level set's own extrema at every step ([STATUS L2435-L2439](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2435-L2439)). The firing location is MEASURED; the undershoot mechanism is DERIVED.
2. The exemption. A growing checkerboard has extrema at its own peaks. On pMesh, 59 % of the cells that the clip must bound are stencil extrema. So the property "is an extremum" does not separate a spurious extremum from a genuine one ([STATUS L2358-L2364](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2358-L2364)). The record reads the separating property as scale; that reading is untested ([STATUS L2380-L2392](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L2380-L2392)).
3. The cone bound. L = 1 is exact only where grad u = 0. Under strain the true Lipschitz constant grows as `L(t) <= L(0) exp(int_0^t ||grad u(s)||_inf ds)`, and a clamp at L = 1 destroys the field ([METHOD L608-L617](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L608-L617)). The bound also admits extrema, so it gives up the damping of a maximum principle and amplifies the grid-scale mode more ([METHOD L564-L569](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L564-L569)). The coupled gain at N = 64 came from the one-step error `u_0`, not from the amplifier `G`. The better distance field gave a better curvature ([METHOD L571-L576](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L571-L576), [[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]). The record gives no mechanism for the loss on uniform translation, where L = 1 is valid ([METHOD L603-L606](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L603-L606)). One way (2026-09-29), the loss is 14.1 times at N = 64. The cone arm also depends on the psi boundary value 0.1 away from the interface: 3.03e-02 with the exact outflow value, 1.02e-01 with zeroGradient. The mechanism is not measured ([METHOD 8.3.4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/aaa0a7dd/METHOD.md#L646-L648)).
4. The limiters. The record gives the orders and no mechanism. The Venkatakrishnan limiter improves the coarse-mesh volume error (N = 32, T = 2: 2.97e-2 to 7.4e-4). The article keeps it off on hexahedra ([MC article L264-L273](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L264-L273)). A limiter on the geometry fit removes the Hessian where the curvature needs it. The curvature plan lists it as a dead end ([PCS L384-L387](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L384-L387)).
5. The structure. Quadratic exactness needs negative weights at an off-node point. So the quadratic value fit has no maximum principle, and a hard limiter collapses its order ([METHOD L786-L792](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L786-L792)).

## The mesh-noise floor is a diagnostic

cfMesh is not bit-reproducible. Two runs of `pMesh` from the identical rendered case give the same point count. In 2658 of 206528 points the last digit differs, at about 3e-12 relative ([CLAUDE L606-L613](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L606-L613)). The mesh digests differ: 7e9ee670c679 against e38b4e8e919b ([advConv3DshearPoly.yaml L39-L48](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/advConv3DshearPoly.yaml#L39-L48)). The null arm runs the same configuration on two independently built meshes and reads the spread of each metric. The record does not state how the spread is normalised. The cone bound turns the 3e-12 change into a 14.2 % volume spread; `none` and `stencilBounds` stay at 0.0 % ([CLAUDE L619-L623](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L619-L623)). The record reads this as a finding about the candidate: the cone bound is unstable to round-off. Report the floor next to every arm difference, and treat a difference at the floor as noise ([CLAUDE L625-L630](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L625-L630), [[concepts/advection-regression-set]]).
