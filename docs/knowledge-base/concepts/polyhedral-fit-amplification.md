# The amplification bound of the fit on polyhedra

> Verdict (2026-09-28). The semi-Lagrangian update is linear in the stencil values, so one step obeys `abs(psi_c^{n+1}) <= Lambda_c max_stencil abs(psi)` with `Lambda_c = abs(1 - sum_j g_j) + sum_j abs(g_j)`, the Lebesgue constant of the fit at the departure displacement ([STATUS L2142-L2146](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2142-L2146), [README L570-L583](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L570-L583)). Measured at `d = -U dt` on the SI Popinet meshes, `Lambda_max` is 1.0527 on blockMesh hexahedra and 1.2608 on pMesh polyhedra, and the whole excess sits in small one-sided cells of 0.32 to 0.33 h ([STATUS L2149-L2161](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2149-L2161)). `Lambda - 1` is proportional to the displacement, so the bound over a fixed physical time, `exp((Lambda - 1) T/dt) = exp(c U T)`, contains no `dt`; the pre-registered dt sweep confirmed it: the sigma = 0 polyhedral control fails at 4.877e-03, 4.660e-03 and 4.558e-03 s at dt, dt/2 and dt/4 ([STATUS L2163-L2175](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2163-L2175), [STATUS L2194-L2220](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2194-L2220)). The defect is polyhedral cells, not cfMesh and not boundary layers: cfMesh's own hexahedral mesher on the same dictionary lands at 1.0549 ([STATUS L2245-L2259](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2245-L2259)). `Lambda` is a one-mesh-pass screen that separates mesh families; only the spectral radius `rho(B)` of the frozen-velocity transport operator decides, and it is above one on every mesh: 1.00441 on production hexahedra, 1.01028 on production pMesh ([METHOD 8.2 L409-L435](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L409-L435)).

## What it is

With `g_j = b(d)^T M^{-1} w_j^2 b(d_j)` for a departure displacement `d`, the fit evaluated at `x_c + d` is `(1 - sum_j g_j) psi_c + sum_j g_j psi_j` ([README L570-L573](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L570-L573)). `Lambda_c = 1` at `d = 0` and whenever every weight is non-negative: the update is then a convex combination and creates no new extremum ([README L579-L581](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L579-L581)). `Lambda_c > 1` is the necessary condition for the checkerboard growth that destroys the far field of a translating polyhedral case ([README L581-L582](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/workflow/README.md#L581-L582)). `Lambda` depends on the stencil geometry alone, so one mesh pass per mesher measures it ([STATUS L2239-L2243](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2239-L2243)).

The bound is necessary, not sufficient. The anchored quadratic at `x_i - C h` on a uniform 1D grid is Lax-Wendroff: `Lambda = 1 + C - C^2 > 1` while `abs(G)^2 = 1 - 4C^2(1 - C^2) sin^4(theta/2) <= 1` ([leiaTestTransportSpectrum.C L32-L44](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/applications/test/leiaTestTransportSpectrum/leiaTestTransportSpectrum.C#L32-L44), [METHOD L471-L474](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L471-L474)). The mechanism is measured instead by the frozen-velocity power iteration: with the velocity frozen and the clip off, the transport stage is an exactly linear operator `B`, and the test reports its spectral radius and its finite-time power norms ([leiaTestTransportSpectrum.C L46-L62](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/applications/test/leiaTestTransportSpectrum/leiaTestTransportSpectrum.C#L46-L62)).

## Why it matters

The sigma = 0 passive control on the polyhedral Popinet mesh keeps an exact uniform stream (max abs u' 2.3e-14) and still grows a fake zero set at step 508, the same step as with surface tension ([STATUS L1822-L1828](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1822-L1828)). The stated mechanism is a checkerboard error amplified by about `(U dt/h)` times the stencil asymmetry per step: 0.5 to 1 % per step in the 0.33 h outlet cells, cancelling on symmetric hexahedral stencils ([STATUS L1830-L1834](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1830-L1834)). A smaller time step cannot remove this failure: it buys proportionally more steps at proportionally smaller growth per step ([STATUS L2169-L2172](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2169-L2172)). The fix must change the update operator, not the step ([STATUS L2217-L2220](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2217-L2220)). The 3D polyhedral kinematic gates never saw it, because their velocity vanishes on the walls where these cells live ([STATUS L1829-L1830](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1829-L1830), [[cases/kinematic-advection-cases]]).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

The polyhedral far field fails because pMesh generates 27 191 cells below 0.4 h, and the five worst amplifiers are 0.32 to 0.33 h one-sided cells; cartesianMesh has none below 0.6 h and snappyHexMesh with three layers none below 0.4 h ([STATUS L2261-L2267](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2261-L2267)). The earlier hypothesis that the danger was cfMesh's boundary-layer grading is retracted by the cell-size histogram ([STATUS L2261-L2262](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2261-L2262)). Better arithmetic cannot help: the amplifying cells are well conditioned, so `householderQR` blew up identically to the normal equations (step-3 phase volume 0.017512193 against 0.017512208) ([STATUS L2276-L2278](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2276-L2278), [[decisions/sl-fit-normal-equations]]). Rank reduction lowers `Lambda`, but the trigger must be `Lambda`, not the singular values ([STATUS L2280-L2286](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2280-L2286)).

## Decisions

- The mesh family of the production method is hexahedral; a hexahedral-family unstructured workflow (cartesianMesh, snappyHexMesh with layers) is clear of the defect ([METHOD 8.1 L400](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L400), [STATUS L2304-L2306](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2304-L2306), [[decisions/mesh-family-hexahedral]]). Snapping costs 36 % in `rho - 1`, so a body-fitted case measures `rho` on its own mesh before a horizon is chosen ([METHOD 8.2 L476-L484](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L476-L484)).
- `Lambda` is a screen that separates mesh families at comparable resolution; it must not rank resolutions of one mesher, nor score a snapped mesh against an aligned one; only `rho` decides ([METHOD 8.2 L433-L435](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L433-L435)).
- The near-wall cells do not get their own step limit; a local time-step constraint cannot repair the failure ([STATUS L2217-L2220](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2217-L2220)).
- No clip and no value bound in production ([[decisions/sl-clip-and-value-bound-off]], [[concepts/value-bounds-and-clips]]).

## Open questions

1. `rho(B) > 1` on every mesh tested, so the acceptance criterion `rho <= 1` of METHOD 9 item 3 is met nowhere ([METHOD L767-L770](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L767-L770)). Hexahedra carry a 2.5x smaller exponent, which over 1563 steps is the difference between a run that completes and one that fails ([METHOD L459-L463](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L459-L463)).
2. The transport-spectrum probe sees no effect of the trace velocity, because a frozen uniform stream makes `reconstruct(flux(U))` return `U` exactly; the recorded projectedFlux gain lives in a solved flux with a pressure correction ([METHOD L447-L455](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L447-L455), [[concepts/trace-velocity-projected-flux]]).
3. A rank choice per cell with `Lambda <= 1` as the trigger is proposed and not built ([STATUS L2284-L2286](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2284-L2286)).
4. The SL pre-print's boundedness paragraph describes a monotone clip as part of the method ([SL article L542-L548](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L542-L548)); production runs with `SL_CLIP false` ([METHOD 8.1 L378](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L378)). The text and the configuration disagree.
5. The first snappyHexMesh arm was vacuous (`minVol 1e-13` removed every extrusion, exit 0) and reproduced blockMesh's `Lambda` to eight digits; verify the artefact, never the exit code ([STATUS L2288-L2295](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L2288-L2295)).
