# Geometric redistancing (GRL), the closed line

> Verdict (2026-09-29; the method-comparison article closed the line in the text of 2df993c, 2026-07-30). The line is closed, and production runs no redistancing ([METHOD 1 L56-L57](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L56-L57), [DP L42-L45](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L42-L45)). The geometric redistancer `planeFootWave` passes the static gate. On a tanh-profiled circle its band error after one event is 4.48e-05 at h = 1/256, with orders 2.54, 2.00 and 1.97 ([static_redistance_orders.tex L15-L18](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/data/tables/static_redistance_orders.tex#L15-L18)). That error, and every static band error of psi below, is a band maximum norm; the record has no L2 or L1 of it. The same event lowers the band mean of `abs(abs(grad psi) - 1)` from 0.537 to 5.1e-3 at N = 64 ([negative deck #/7/1](https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/decks/geometrically-redistanced-level-set-negative-results.html#/7/1)). On an exact plane the event is exact to 1.61e-13 ([redistanceStatic2D_orders.tex L40-L43](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/data/tables/redistanceStatic2D_orders.tex#L40-L43)). The comparison fills fail the gate. `anchoredEikonal` is first order in the band on a plane (1.84e-03, order 1.00). PDE reinitialisation raises the band error at every resolution ([T2 L30-L33](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/data/tables/redistanceStatic2D_orders.tex#L30-L33), [T1 L5-L8](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/data/tables/static_redistance_orders.tex#L5-L8)). Transport closes the line. On the reversed vortex the criterion fires every step, and the volume error of `planeFootWave` runs to about 6 at every resolution ([vortexThresholdGRL.yaml L4-L9](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/vortexThresholdGRL.yaml#L4-L9), [bulkVortexGRL_errors.csv](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/data/tables/bulkVortexGRL_errors.csv)). The frozen-band variant has zero interface displacement by construction. It still raises the volume error from 0.017 to 1.77 at N = 256 on the T = 8 Eulerian vortex ([MC article sec:frozen L315-L325](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L315-L325)). The reason is general: a redistancer rebuilds distance to every zero set it gets, advection noise included ([MC article L327-L341](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L327-L341)).

## What it is

The GRL method restores the signed-distance property geometrically, from the planes the Detrixhe-Aslam phase indicator already reconstructs ([GRL article L50-L65](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L50-L65), [[models/phase-indicator]]). The Eulerian solver `leiaRedistancedLevelSetFoam` advects the level set in the form `ddt(psi) + div(v psi) - psi div(v) = 0` ([GRL article L86-L95](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L86-L95)). One event of `planeFootWave` has four parts ([GRL article sec:algorithm L310-L334](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L310-L334), `sec:algorithm`):

1. Plane per band cell. Each sign-change cell fits `psi_l = n_c . x + d_c` by least squares over itself and its face neighbours. The event normalises the plane: `delta_c(x) = (n_c . x + d_c)/abs(n_c)` ([L97-L133](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L97-L133), `sec:llsplanes`).
2. Anchors. A band cell keeps its own plane distance and a foot point `f_c = x_c - D_c n_c`. The first ring gets the plicRDF-weighted mean of the adjacent donor planes ([L135-L166](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L135-L166), `sec:anchors`).
3. Fill. A `FaceCellWave` carries the nearest donor to every other cell. The cell receives the continuous plane distance `psi_new = sign(psi_old) abs((x_c - f) . n)`, not the distance to the foot point ([L222-L258](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L222-L258), `sec:wave`).
4. Trigger. An event fires when the band mean of `abs(abs(grad psi) - 1)` over `abs(psi) < 6h` exceeds `(h/L)^2` ([L336-L340](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L336-L340)).

Two comparison fills share the anchors. `anchoredEikonal` solves Tucker's advection-diffusion form of the steady Eikonal equation, and `PDE` solves the Sussman-Smereka-Osher pseudo-time equation ([anchoredEikonalRedistancer.H L29-L48](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/redistancer/anchoredEikonalRedistancer.H#L29-L48), `src/leiaLevelSet/redistancer/anchoredEikonalRedistancer.H`; [pdeRedistancer.H L28-L36](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/redistancer/pdeRedistancer.H#L28-L36)). The frozen-band variant of `PDE` keeps the sign-change band and two guard layers at their transported values. It refills the bulk only ([pdeRedistancer.H L79-L96](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/src/leiaLevelSet/redistancer/pdeRedistancer.H#L79-L96), [DP L488-L500](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L488-L500)). The members, keys and tokens are in [[models/redistancer]]; the document is [[studies/grl-pre-print]] (pre-print: https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/preprints/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.pdf).

## Why it matters

Transport without reinitialisation does not keep the distance property: the band `abs(grad psi)` spreads to 0.84-1.37 ([METHOD 9 L761-L762](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L761-L762)). The GRL line asked whether a rare geometric event can restore the property without a cost at the interface. The answer set the method. No event may rewrite the band. Source terms that vanish on the zero set keep the signed-distance property instead ([PCT L599-L604](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-combined-source-terms.md#L599-L604), [[hubs/gradient-control]]). The pass criterion of the line is "a redistance event must not injure the field" ([GRL article L450-L452](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L450-L452), `sec:negative`).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

1. Every band rewrite moves the interface. The zero set and the band values are the same degrees of freedom. The plane anchor is a chord of the curved interface. So each event shifts the interface by `O(h^2 kappa)`, always with the same sign. The shifts add up over the events ([MC article L238-L241](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L238-L241), [GRL article L146-L148](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L146-L148), [L405-L407](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L405-L407), [PCS dead end 7 L378-L381](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L378-L381)). DERIVED, with the one-sign shift MEASURED in `E_vol`.
2. The criterion does not keep events rare. The fill's own post-event floor lies about 20 times above the automatic threshold, so the criterion fires every step. Every-step firing times the per-event shift gives the volume runaway ([vortexThresholdGRL.yaml L4-L9](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/vortexThresholdGRL.yaml#L4-L9), [main deck #/5/3](https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/decks/geometrically-redistanced-level-set.html#/5/3)). MEASURED.
3. A redistancer rebuilds distance to every zero set it gets. The Eulerian `linearUpwind` transport leaves bounded over- and undershoots, which are spurious sign changes in the bulk. The frozen band keeps the true interface. The bulk fill then builds distance to the spurious crossings, and the indicator reads them as phase ([MC article L327-L341](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L327-L341)). The quadratic semi-Lagrangian fit has no maximum principle either, so the same argument holds there ([METHOD 9 L790-L794](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L790-L794), [[concepts/value-bounds-and-clips]]). MEASURED on the Eulerian line; DERIVED for the semi-Lagrangian line.
4. Each comparison fill has its own defect. The distance to a foot-point cloud is a union of cones. Its level sets bulge between the feet, and its gradient kinks in the band that the trigger measures ([negative deck #/3/2](https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/decks/geometrically-redistanced-level-set-negative-results.html#/3/2)). The Eikonal fill carries first-order upwind transport plus a regularisation bias into the outer band cells ([negative deck #/4/3](https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/decks/geometrically-redistanced-level-set-negative-results.html#/4/3)). A fixed pseudo-step exceeds the pseudo-CFL limit on a fine mesh; the article calls this "a mesh-dependent stability bomb" ([GRL article L463-L467](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L463-L467)). With a safe step, the central Hamiltonian still oscillates on the saturated tanh profile ([negative deck #/5/2](https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/decks/geometrically-redistanced-level-set-negative-results.html#/5/2)). MEASURED.

## Decisions

- `REDISTANCER noRedistancing` in the global default; no reinitialisation at any point of the production method ([DP L42-L45](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/cases/default.parameter#L42-L45), [METHOD 1 L56-L57](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/METHOD.md#L56-L57)).
- Dead ends on record: plane-based band rewrite at any cadence ([PCS L378-L381](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L378-L381)), redistancing of any kind at any cadence ([PCT L599-L604](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-combined-source-terms.md#L599-L604)). The roadmap forbids every-step redistancing and requires that the zero-set and phase-volume errors do not worsen ([RM L536-L550](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L536-L550)).
- Binding rule of the author, 2026-08-07: no study modifies or suspends the psi advection for a static case. A reset candidate must pass the kinematic suite and a moving coupled gate before promotion ([PCS L323-L333](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L323-L333), [[cases/kinematic-advection-cases]]).
- Five standing rules of the line ([negative deck #/7/2](https://leia-openfoam.github.io/leia/decks/geometrically-redistanced-level-set-negative-results.html#/7/2)):
  1. an event must not injure the field;
  2. the trigger policy comes after the fill quality;
  3. assert each model on its contract region only;
  4. use mesh-relative parameters only;
  5. take distances from continuous geometry, not from point clouds.
