# The linear semi-Lagrangian line

> Verdict (2026-09-28). The linear line keeps the quadratic line's departure foot and phase indicator and changes one ingredient, the reconstruction at the foot ([LSL article L50-L57](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L50-L57)). The least-squares linear value fit, which the article calls `nestedLSQ`, converges on the reversed 2D vortex at CFL 1/2: shape order 1.070, volume 1.452, band gradient 2.082 over seven rungs ([lsl_convergence_orders.csv](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/data/tables/lsl_convergence_orders.csv), [LSL article L426-L429](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L426-L429)). It is only conditionally stable: at CFL 1 it destabilises at N >= 128, and the 3D hexahedral shear runs away past N = 50 with a gradient defect of order 1e5 at N = 128 ([L433-L441](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L433-L441), [L481-L489](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L481-L489)). The raw Taylor extrapolation `linearTaylor` diverges in pure advection, gradient defect 1.4e9 at N = 128 and 2.0e21 at N = 256 ([L265-L285](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L265-L285)). The 3D deformation converges at 2.28 (hex) and 1.52 (poly) ([L477-L480](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L477-L480)). The line is documented as a low-cost method; the quadratic value fit is production ([[models/sl-reconstruction]]).

## What it is

The linear value fit is the linear-basis restriction of the quadratic fit: `R_c(x) = psi_c + g_c . (x - x_c)` with `g_c` the weighted least-squares slope over the arrival cell's stencil, weights `1/abs(d_i)`, constant dropped ([LSL article L197-L214](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L197-L214), [linearWeightedLeastSquaresReconstruction.H L29-L41](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/linearWeightedLeastSquaresReconstruction.H#L29-L41)). The per-cell system is 2x2 in 2D and 3x3 in 3D, with no cached pseudo-inverse, so the memory is O(1) per cell ([L233-L239](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L233-L239)). The fit is exact for linear fields, so the per-step error is O(h^2) and the accumulated error over `T/dt` steps is first order ([L216-L221](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L216-L221)).

`linearTaylor` evaluates the arrival cell's own point-cell least-squares gradient at the foot, `psi_c + (grad psi)_c . (x_d - x_c)`; with the semi-Lagrangian foot this reproduces first-order upwind ([L243-L250](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L243-L250), [linearTaylorReconstruction.H L29-L37](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/linearTaylorReconstruction.H#L29-L37)). It injects a differentiated field, and that is the mechanism of its failure ([iDEC report L220-L221](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/supplementary/iDEC_failure_report.tex#L220-L221), [[concepts/idec-defect-correction-failure]]).

A naming trap: the code renamed the reconstruction formerly called `nestedLSQ` to `quadraticTaylor`, which builds a quadratic from twice-differentiated psi and needs the clip to stay bounded ([quadraticTaylorReconstruction.H L51-L53](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/quadraticTaylorReconstruction.H#L51-L53), [RM L24-L28](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L24-L28)). The LSL article uses `nestedLSQ` for the linear value fit, and its study sweeps `linearWeightedLeastSquares` ([linearConv2Dvortex.yaml L28](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/config/linearConv2Dvortex.yaml#L28)); the curated table carries `nestedLSQ` in its reconstruction column ([lsl_convergence_orders.csv](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/data/tables/lsl_convergence_orders.csv)). See the open questions.

## Why it matters

The line shows that stencil averaging, not the polynomial degree alone, makes a semi-Lagrangian reconstruction stable ([LSL article L665-L669](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L665-L669)). It also fixes the second load-bearing property of the production fit, degree at least two: a linear value fit drives `abs(grad psi) - 1` to 1e21 ([METHOD 2.2 L118-L121](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L118-L121)). In the coupled solver the transport order is not the limiting factor: nine matched pairs of quadratic against `linearTaylor` transport agree to 3 to 4 digits in every filtered arm ([STATUS L503-L518](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L503-L518)); those arms predate the psi-filter seam bug ([[retractions/psi-filter-seam-bug]]). The article records the same two-phase reading: `linearTaylor` served the consistent-linear pipeline, where the coupled dissipation and the short horizon kept it in its stable regime ([LSL article L287-L298](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L287-L298)).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

The single-cell extrapolation across the foot displacement overshoots where `abs(grad psi)` is already too steep and steepens it further; there is no stencil averaging to damp it, and finer meshes take more steps, so the runaway is worse under refinement ([LSL article L257-L260](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L257-L260)). The least-squares slope defers the same feedback rather than removing it: the sustained, non-reversing strain of the 3D shear steepens the gradient until the averaged slope can no longer damp it, so the safe resolution is flow-dependent ([L489-L504](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L489-L504)).

## Decisions

- Production transport is the quadratic value fit; the linear fit is a documented low-cost line for CFL <= 1/2 ([LSL article L615-L620](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L615-L620), [[decisions/sl-reconstruction-uncached-qwls]]).
- `linearTaylor` must never be used for reinitialisation-free advection ([L599-L601](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L599-L601)).

## Open questions

1. Which linear member is unstable without the clip? The article calls the linear value fit stable at CFL 1/2; the study header that sweeps `linearWeightedLeastSquares` records it as unstable in advection without the clip ([linearConv2Dvortex.yaml L35-L37](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/config/linearConv2Dvortex.yaml#L35-L37)); METHOD attributes 1e21 to "a linear value fit" ([METHOD L120-L121](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L120-L121)) and the article attributes 2.0e21 to `linearTaylor`. The table's `nestedLSQ` label does not resolve it.
2. The six `linearConv*` studies ran on four ranks before the gradU coupled-patch fix and are on the contaminated list ([STATUS L626-L629](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L626-L629), [[retractions/gradu-coupled-patch-contamination]]). No re-run is on record.
3. The polyhedral shear result depends on the stencil-bounds clip, which the quadratic line has since falsified as a fix ([LSL article L306-L312](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L306-L312), [[concepts/value-bounds-and-clips]]).
