# The normal-projected semi-Lagrangian line (closed)

> Verdict (2026-09-28, gate of 2026-08-03). The normal-projected update replaces the value resampling `psi^n(x_d)` by a geometric update `psi^{n+1}(x_c) = d_c + delta`: the foot is traced along `u_n = (u.n)n`, `d_c` is the signed offset of the cell centre to the zero set of its own quadratic fit, and `delta` is the normal displacement of the trace ([nPSL article L191-L209](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L191-L209)). The trace itself is clean: 0.017 h band error after one step and baseline-equal transport over 1600 steps of rigid translation ([L403-L409](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L403-L409)). The write-back is not: three offset engines of very different accuracy give the same band error of about 1.11 h after 100 steps, x1.7 per 10 steps, independent of dt ([L411-L431](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L411-L431)). The corrugation hypothesis is falsified: the m > 4 mode grows to 0.223 h against 0.209 h for `pointValue` at 16 h of displacement, because the noise re-enters through the fitted normals ([L439-L448](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L439-L448)). On the reversed vortex the `strain` mode is anti-convergent (order -1.24) and the geometric mode stagnates (order -0.17) against 2.9 for `pointValue` ([L456-L468](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L456-L468)). Do not promote it to the production trajectory ([RM L525-L531](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L525-L531)).

## What it is

The continuous argument is exact: the tangential velocity satisfies `u_t . grad psi = 0` identically, so psi is constant along trajectories of `u_n` for any field, drifted or not ([nPSL article L102-L130](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L102-L130)). The discrete scheme takes `n = g/abs(g)` from the quadratic value fit of `psi^n`, traces the departure-centred second-order foot with `u_n^n` and the stored `u_n^{n-1}` (projected with the previous step's normals), and assembles `grad u_n` analytically from the fit and the solver's `grad u` ([L132-L189](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L132-L189), [normalProjectedScheme.H L63-L78](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/normalProjectedScheme.H#L63-L78)). Three renormalisations exist ([DP L269-L272](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/cases/default.parameter#L269-L272)):

| `renormalization` | in-band update | offset engine | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `geometric` | `d_c + delta`, `d_c` from the stable quadratic root `2 psi_c / (abs(g) + sqrt(D))`, `D = abs(g)^2 - 2 psi_c h_nn`, accepted while `D >= beta abs(g)^2`, `beta = 1/4` | `rayRoot` or the stabilized `footPoint` | [L211-L260](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L211-L260) |
| `strain` (code default) | `(psi_c + delta abs(g)) (1 + eps_nn dt)`, from `D abs(grad psi)/Dt = -abs(grad psi) eps_nn` | none | [L286-L309](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L286-L309) |
| `none` | raw transport along the ray, `psi_c + delta abs(g)` | none | [L275-L280](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L275-L280) |

The update ladder gates the renormalising branch to the band (`abs(psi_c)/abs(g)` within one stencil radius), transports the far field raw, and freezes degenerate cells ([L262-L284](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L262-L284)). The scheme uses no corrector and no clip: a renormalised value may legitimately leave the stencil range of a drifted psi ([normalProjectedScheme.H L86-L89](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/semiLagrangian/normalProjectedScheme.H#L86-L89)). Selected by `scheme normalProjected`, token `SL_SCHEME` ([[models/sl-scheme]]).

A related but distinct diagnostic is the `pointValue` trajectory `normalProjection`, which inserts `(u.n)n` into the Taylor foot but keeps the value resampling ([[models/sl-scheme]]). Under the integral force it gave the latest translating runaway (0.04365 s against 0.01309 s for `normalClosestPoint`) while being physically invalid by t = 0.03 (max abs U 0.248 for U0 = 0.05 m/s) ([RM L136-L155](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L136-L155)); at fixed Courant number its volume orders are 1.45 and 0.81 against 2.47 and 2.55 for the physical velocity ([RM L302-L327](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L302-L327)).

## Why it matters

The design targeted two measured properties of the baseline: the grid-scale corrugation of the zero set under rigid translation, and the collapse of the band gradient that precedes the parasitic-current growth ([nPSL article L81-L96](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L81-L96), [[concepts/polyhedral-fit-amplification]]). The gate result changed the reading of the corrugation instability: it is a property of consulting the local fit at all, not of the value path, so estimator- and scheme-independent cures must target the fit ([L443-L448](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L443-L448), [[concepts/curvature-corrugation-and-the-fit]]). The write-back failure is also the reason the immediate-target multiplicative corrector of the combined-source note is not a separate route: `delta(psi/g) = -psi delta g/g^2` is the same amplifier ([PCT L167-L187](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-combined-source-terms.md#L167-L187), [[concepts/sl-source-step]]).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

Writing any fit-derived offset back into psi feeds neighbour noise into the next step's fit at order one per step: `delta d_c ~ psi_c delta abs(g) / abs(g)^2` carries no factor of dt, so the offset engine's accuracy is irrelevant to the loop gain ([nPSL article L425-L431](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L425-L431)). A grid-scale wiggle in psi perturbs `g`, hence `n`, hence both the trace direction and the written increment, with the same per-displacement gain as the resampling it replaced ([L443-L446](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L443-L446)). The strain mode carries a factor dt in its noise gain, and it still fails on a deforming flow: its volume error grows to O(1) and the band minimum of `abs(grad psi)` goes to 0 at every resolution ([L470-L474](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L470-L474)).

## Decisions

- The value path remains the most accurate transport measured; every geometric substitute is worse on deforming flows ([L481-L484](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L481-L484)). The pre-registered acceptance rule (closed-loop amplification <= 1 at lambda about 2 h) is not met, so the ladders and the droplet runs of the design plan were not run ([L446-L448](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L446-L448)).
- What survives: the normal-projected trace, the shared `quadraticRoot` offset conversion for the curvature path, the stabilized foot point as a geometric kernel, and the strain mode as the only renormalisation with an O(dt) noise gain ([L485-L491](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/normal-projected-semi-lagrangian/npsl-article/normalProjectedSemiLagrangian.tex#L485-L491)).
- The multiplicative corrector `psi <- psi g_tar/g` is a listed dead end ([PCT L610-L613](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-combined-source-terms.md#L610-L613)).
