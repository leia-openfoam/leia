# Eulerian FV transport of the level set

> Verdict (2026-09-28). The Eulerian line transports psi by an implicit finite-volume equation in the advective (Hamilton-Jacobi) form, `ddt(psi) + div(phi psi) - psi div(phi) = S`, with a deferred-correction loop so that the explicit part of the second-order `linearUpwind` divergence converges within the step ([eulerianAdvection.H L29-L43](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/advection/eulerianAdvection.H#L29-L43)). On the reversed vortex at 512^2 and T = 8 it reaches a shape error of 3.04e-04 in 1170 s, against 1.49e-05 in 595 s for the quadratic semi-Lagrangian line: 20 times the error at twice the wall clock ([benchVortex_decision.tex](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/data/tables/benchVortex_decision.tex), [MC article L194-L200](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L194-L200)). It is the robust second: plain defect-corrected transport is bounded and second order, its `linearUpwind` diffusion erodes `abs(grad psi)`, and the loop costs 2 to 3 assemblies per step ([MC article L220-L224](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L220-L224)). Bounding the semi-Lagrangian scheme the Eulerian way does not work: a Barth-Jespersen limiter collapses its order from 3.0 to 0.1 and Venkatakrishnan to 0.9 ([L212-L219](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L212-L219), [L264-L273](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L264-L273)), and the conservative flux form loses 17 % of the phase volume by t = 6.5 at N = 128 ([L147-L166](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L147-L166)).

## What it is

The kinematic solver `leiaLevelSetFoam` selects the line with `levelSet.advection.type eulerian` ([[models/level-set-advection]]). The equation composes the runtime-selected velocity extension (the advecting flux `phiExt`, identity for `none`) and the SDPLS source (a right-hand side that vanishes on the zero level set, `noSource` off) ([eulerianAdvection.H L41-L43](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/advection/eulerianAdvection.H#L41-L43), [eulerianAdvection.C L78-L97](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/advection/eulerianAdvection.C#L78-L97)). The advective form is used because the conservative form equals it only for a discretely solenoidal flux, and a residual discrete divergence of the prescribed or intermediate flux must not act as a spurious compression source ([GRL article L86-L95](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/geometrically-redistanced-levelset/grl-level-set-article/geometricallyRedistancedLevelSet.tex#L86-L95), [alphaEqn.H L8-L11](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/applications/solvers/leiaLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/alphaEqn.H#L8-L11)). The number of deferred-correction passes is `nDefCorr` (default 3) in `controlDict` ([eulerianAdvection.C L90-L92](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/src/leiaLevelSet/advection/eulerianAdvection.C#L90-L92)); the divergence scheme is the case token `DIV`, whose axis spans `linear`, `linearUpwind`, `vanLeer`, `Gamma`, `MUSCL`, `Minmod`, `OSPRE`, `SFCD`, `SuperBee`, `UMIST`, `vanAlbada` and `upwind` ([2Dvortex.parameter](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/cases/2Dvortex.parameter)).

The Eulerian two-phase solver `leiaLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam` carries the same form, re-assembled on every PIMPLE outer corrector, with the band refresh, the criterion-gated redistancing and the volume correction once per step on the final corrector ([alphaEqn.H L14-L33](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/applications/solvers/leiaLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/alphaEqn.H#L14-L33), [L44-L60](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/applications/solvers/leiaLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/alphaEqn.H#L44-L60)). Its mass flux was frozen until 2026-09-27 ([[concepts/eulerian-solver-mass-flux-port]]).

## Why it matters

An Eulerian discretisation couples the interface accuracy to the flux limiter and to the divergence scheme; the semi-Lagrangian update has no flux, no divergence and no linear system ([LSL article L99-L106](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/linear-semi-lagrangian-level-set/lsl-level-set-article/linearSemiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L99-L106)). The comparison ran every line through one dictionary-configured solver at one decomposition (np 4) and one reporting cadence, so the wall clocks compare method to method ([MC article L69-L82](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L69-L82)). The verdict gives each line its place: the semi-Lagrangian quadratic scheme where the interface is resolved, the Eulerian scheme as the robust second, the SDPLS sources for volume conservation under maximal stretching on coarse meshes and for two-phase coupling, the velocity extension dominated ([L194-L205](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L194-L205)). The under-resolution death of the T = 8 filament at N <= 64 is method-independent; refinement, not gradient maintenance, is the cure ([L204-L205](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L204-L205)).

## Decisions

- Given sufficient resolution, choose the quadratic semi-Lagrangian transport; the Eulerian line is the robust second ([MC article L194-L203](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L194-L203), [METHOD 8 L343-L347](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/METHOD.md#L343-L347)).
- A higher-order bounded flux (WENO or limited cubic) is the named fix for the Eulerian diffusion; it is not built ([MC article L222-L224](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/method-comparison/method-comparison-article/methodComparison.tex#L222-L224)).
- The Eulerian two-phase solver is the `baselineEulerian` gate candidate; it cannot yet run the SL curvature extension ([STATUS L3703-L3710](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L3703-L3710)).
