# The parasitic-current mechanism: source and amplifiers

> Verdict (2026-09-28). Every endpoint of the stationary and translating droplet factorises as `max|U|(T) = u0(h) exp(G(h))`, with `u0` the one-step kick and `G` the e-folds accumulated to time `T` ([STATUS 4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L991-L1005)). The source `u0` is the curvature-estimator error: the step-1 disturbance is independent of the translation speed to 0.6 percent over a fourfold change in `U0`, and the exact curvature `kappa = 1/R` removes it by a factor `1.27e6` ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L79-L102)). The amplifier `G` grows with `U0` (the `U0 = 0` and `U0 = 0.05` arms separate by a factor 67 in `L1` over the horizon) and with the density ratio (all eight arms at ratio 838.8 destabilise between 8427 and 9987 steps, all eight at ratio 1 complete) ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L113-L125)). The exact-curvature arms stay bounded at every combination, so no amplifier independent of the source exists: the curvature estimator is the whole story ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L190-L219)). Under refinement the kick shrinks about 11 times per doubling while the exponent grows 1.5 to 2.1 times, `G ~ h^-3.27` in 3D and `h^-0.95` then `h^-0.56` in 2D; currents converge only while `G < 3.5/p` ([PSH 0d](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L294-L324)). The discrete pump is the phase-coherent response of the refitted curvature to the displaced interface: clamping its amplitude 460 times does not stop the growth, freezing the curvature does ([PSH 0g](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L864-L889)).

## What it is

The identity `u_end = u0 exp(gAvg nSteps)`, with `gAvg = ln(u_end/u0)/nSteps`, holds for every arm by construction and predicts the endpoint from the first CSV row ([PSH 0b](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L75-L83)). The kick scales as the force error times the step over which it acts, `h^2 h^1.5 = h^3.5`, and it does: `h^+3.62, +3.51, +3.69` in 2D and `h^+4.22, +2.91, +3.30` in 3D ([PSH 0d](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L274-L292)). The exponent goes the other way, and its slope `p` in `G ~ h^-p` is the number a fix has to change; `p` is scoreable at a quarter horizon, four times cheaper than `t_blow` ([PSH 0d](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L301-L324)). With the psi filter off the per-step amplification `A` changes sign between R/h = 12.7 and 15.8 in 3D, so the stability boundary belongs to the amplifier, not to the filter ([PSH 0d](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L252-L264)); after the seam fix the gain changes sign with resolution in both dimensions, near R/h = 20 in 2D and 14 in 3D ([PSH 0c](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L197-L201)).

The mode picture. On the stationary droplet at N = 128 the unstable content is the `m = 2` capillary mode: `r_2 = +18.8` 1/s on the near-clean profile where the same mode at N = 64 is damped at `-11` 1/s, `m = 6` is damped everywhere, and profile drift raises the coupling coefficient by 55 percent but does not trigger ([PCS 18.3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1523-L1552)). Halving the step halves the rate: `r_2 = 18.8, 12.7, 8.01, 4.02` 1/s, Richardson intercept `+0.03` 1/s, so the semi-discrete loop is neutral and the instability is the explicit time coupling of the force to that mode, rate `c dt` ([PCS 18.4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-curvature-stabilization.md#L1554-L1580)). The velocity oscillation of the filter-off 2D twin is the `m = 8` capillary wave, period 1.56 ms against 1.455 ms predicted, with an envelope of -97 to +212 1/s against the physical viscous decay of -128 1/s ([PSH 0e](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L446-L483)). The first explanation, an explicit force anti-damping at `+omega^2 dt/2`, is retracted: the force is built from `psi^{n+1}`, the map is symplectic Euler with `|lambda| = 1`, and the growth is the net work per cycle of a mesh-locked curvature error ([PSH 0e](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L530-L579), [[retractions/force-at-n-not-n-plus-1]], [[concepts/force-time-centring]]).

What the current does: on the translating droplet the disturbance starts as an exactly zero-mean multipole and organises into a coherent, momentum-conserving propulsion of the droplet, 13 percent of `U0` by 60 ms, with the fluctuation flat ([SL article `sec:translating`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2030-L2056)). The discrete force does net work on the interface, which is the design target of [[concepts/variational-capillary-force]].

## Why it matters

The decomposition names the target of every lever: implicitness attacks `G`, variational pairing attacks `u0` ([CLAUDE](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/CLAUDE.md#L414-L421)). It also explains why a delivery that improves the force by two orders and leaves the gain alone buys no stability ([PSH 5](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L1012-L1020), [[concepts/cell-centre-inverse-curvature]]), and why the trace of the semi-Lagrangian foot with the projected face flux, a parameter-free operator, turns the N = 128 growth of +118 1/s into a decay of -52 1/s: 70 percent of that is the reconstruct operator, 30 percent solenoidality ([STATUS 4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1070-L1147), [[concepts/trace-velocity-projected-flux]]).

## Why it failed, or why we think so

Nothing in the force chain except the curvature carries the defect. The curvature error is a mesh-locked function of the interface position ([[concepts/curvature-from-the-fit]]); the balanced force absorbs its uniform part and passes its variation ([[concepts/balanced-force-csf-flux]]); the current displaces the interface; the fit re-reads the displaced interface and returns an error correlated with the displacement. A symplectic map conserves energy only for a force that derives from a potential, and this force does not ([PSH 0e](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L571-L579)). Translation and the density ratio do not create the current; translation stops it from relaxing and the light phase turns the same force residual into a larger velocity ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L104-L117)).

## Decisions

- Velocity errors are reported as volume-weighted `L1` and `L2` relative to `U_ref = 0.05` m/s, never `L_inf` ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L194-L198), [[concepts/error-vector-and-read-out-instants]]).
- Candidates are scored on the exponent `p` at a quarter horizon, and `gAvg` is compared only at equal step counts ([PSH 0d](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L321-L324), [STATUS 4](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L1025-L1028)).
- The variational capillary force is the target; no amplifier independent of the source needs attacking first ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/8867581/STATUS.md#L217-L219)).
