# The variational capillary force (proposal)

> Verdict (2026-09-29). The variational capillary force is a proposal, not a result ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2504-L2509) `sec:variational`). It takes the discrete surface energy `sigma A_h`, with `A_h = sum_c V_c |g_c|`, and derives the force from it, so the force does zero net work around any closed cycle of states ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2533-L2563), [CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L444-L451)). The record names it the right target: with the exact curvature every arm of the amplifier gate stays bounded, so the curvature estimator is the whole source ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L195-L224)). The Shannon audit found that interFoam is bounded by the pairing of operator, transport and state, not by accuracy ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L594-L612)). The interFoam operator alone runs away in all eight arms inside leia ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L658-L678)). A clamp that caps the curvature error 460 times does not stop the growth, and a frozen curvature does, so the pump is the live curvature refill ([PSH 0g](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L807-L889)). No implementation exists at d1e3414: no model, no token and no gate; the config `variationalForce2D` runs the interFoam operator, not this proposal ([`variationalForce2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/variationalForce2D.yaml#L1-L54)). The proposal stands or falls on one question: whether the pressure projection realises the volume-constraint multiplier exactly ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2628-L2635)).

## What it is

The construction of the article, in four steps ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2533-L2585)):

1. The discrete area is the co-area functional of the fields the solver has: `A_h = sum_c V_c |g_c|`, with the Gauss gradient `g_c = (1/V_c) sum_{f in F(c)} alpha_f S_f` and `alpha_f` the face interpolate of the phase indicator. For a sharp interface the integral of `|grad alpha|` is the interface area.
2. The force must deliver exactly the energy that the surface releases, for every velocity field: `sum_c V_c f_c . u_c = -sigma dA_h/dt`, with `d alpha_c/dt = -(u . grad_h alpha)_c`.
3. Matching the coefficients of `u_c` gives the CSF form with a new curvature: `f_c = sigma kappa_var,c (grad_h alpha)_c`, with `kappa_var,c = -(1/V_c) dA_h/d alpha_c`. The balanced-force delivery stays; only the curvature changes.
4. In closed form, `kappa_var,c = -(1/V_c) sum_{f in F(c)} (d alpha_f/d alpha_c) (n_O(f) - n_N(f)) . S_f`, with `n_c = g_c/|g_c|`. It is a divergence of the unit normal, built from the jump of the cell-centred normal across each face with the interpolation weights of the gradient.

The CLAUDE.md worked example writes the variation through the level set, `f_c = -sigma dA_h/dpsi_c` ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L447-L451)). The article derives it through `alpha_c` and lists the route through `psi` as open question (ii) ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2637-L2648)).

## Why it matters

1. The measurements ask for a structural property, not for accuracy. The exact curvature removes the one-step disturbance by 1.3e6. The residual the pressure cannot absorb is, on a uniform mesh, the alpha-weighted face-normal gradient of the curvature, so the driver differentiates `kappa`. The disturbance organises into a momentum-conserving propulsion, which is net work over a cycle ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2511-L2531), [[concepts/parasitic-current-mechanism]]).
2. interFoam shows what a pairing does. With the conjugate interpolation the capillary power collapses on any mesh to `P_sigma = -sigma sum_c K_c V_c alphadot_c`, a discrete time derivative of an interface functional. The condition is that the same alpha builds `K`, weights `snGrad` and is advected in flux form by the projected flux. MULES then keeps the state in a compact set ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L598-L612)).
3. leia breaks that pairing three times ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L638-L647)). `psi` moves pointwise by the semi-Lagrangian trace. The force weight is the `snGrad` of a separate geometric alpha. `kappa` comes from the fit and the parallel-surface inverse, which have no generating functional.
4. It is the only route that is a theorem and not an inequality. Every other mitigation (amplitude limit, operator choice, time centring, coupling iteration, convective scheme, momentum time order) is eliminated by measurement ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L698-L703), [PSH 0g](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L885-L889)). Filters are not allowed as a fix ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L426-L442), [[decisions/psi-filter-none]]).
5. It meets the unstructured-FVM constraint by construction: a sum over cells and a loop over their faces, a face-neighbour stencil and one halo exchange of the normal ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2610-L2615)).

What it would not buy is accuracy: `kappa_var` may be less accurate than the fitted curvature, and first order is the expectation ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2617-L2622), [SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2659-L2664)).

## Where in the code

No implementation exists at d1e3414. A search of `src/` and `applications/` finds no variational model, no `kappa_var` and no discrete area functional. The pieces that exist:

1. The balanced-force CSF face flux that the proposal keeps; only its curvature input would change ([[concepts/balanced-force-csf-flux]], [[models/surface-tension-force]]).
2. The work diagnostic. `capillaryResidualPower = (1/dt) sum_c rho_c V_c U_c . dU_c`, where `dU = rAU reconstruct((phig - p_rghEqn.flux())/rAUf)` is the increment the pressure did not absorb; divided by the disturbance kinetic energy `E = sum_c 0.5 rho_c V_c |U_c - U_ref|^2` it is the growth rate `P/E` in 1/s ([`writeDropletMetrics.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/writeDropletMetrics.H#L299-L323), [`createDropletMetricsFile.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/createDropletMetricsFile.H#L15)).
3. The two diagnostic hooks of PSH 0g: `SL_FREEZE_KAPPA` ([`createSLFields.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/createSLFields.H#L162-L179), [`slAlphaEqn.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/slAlphaEqn.H#L238-L241)) and `levelSet.curvatureClamp` ([`createSLFields.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/createSLFields.H#L452-L472), [`slAlphaEqn.H`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/applications/solvers/leiaSemiLagrangianLevelSetTwoPhaseFoam/slAlphaEqn.H#L372-L381)). The configs of the two runs, `kappaClamp2D` and `freezeKappa2D`, are not in `config/` at d1e3414.
4. `config/variationalForce2D.yaml` selects `divGradAlphaSnGradAlpha`, interFoam's curvature inside leia ([`variationalForce2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/variationalForce2D.yaml#L46-L54)). The name refers to the pairing hypothesis, not to this construction.

## Why it failed, or why we think so

The proposal is not run, so it has not failed. Two partial routes have failed. The interFoam operator without the interFoam transport and state keeps the loop unbounded, because leia manufactures a sharp alpha from an unconstrained `psi` every step ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L670-L678)). A saturating but non-variational delivery is killed by the clamp run ([PSH 0g](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L816-L822)). The header of `variationalForce2D.yaml` calls interFoam's curvature the variational derivative of `E_h = sum_c V_c |grad_h a|` ([`variationalForce2D.yaml`](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/config/variationalForce2D.yaml#L9-L18)); the later audit corrects this, because the Jacobian is not symmetric ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L616-L622)).

## Decisions

- The variational capillary force is the target of the capillary line; no amplifier independent of the source needs attacking first ([STATUS 0](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/STATUS.md#L222-L224)).
- It is not implemented. The article asks for theoretical work on five points before an implementation is worth its cost ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2624-L2626)).

## Open questions

1. (i) The volume constraint: does the range of the discrete gradient contain the constraint direction `dV_h/d alpha`? If yes, the projection realises the multiplier, and the constrained stationary point is an exact equilibrium. If no, a residual of the same kind survives ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2628-L2635)).
2. (ii) The chain through the level set: alpha is rebuilt from `psi` by the plane-fit indicator, and `psi` is resampled, not advected by a flux. The energy identity holds for the force, not automatically for the transport; the article calls this the most consequential point ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2637-L2648), [[models/phase-indicator]]).
3. (iii) Differentiability: `|g_c|` is not differentiable at `g_c = 0`, which needs a band restriction and no epsilon; the plane-fit `alpha(psi)` is not C1 where the cut topology of a cell changes ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2650-L2657)).
4. (iv) The consistency order of `A_h` and `kappa_var`, expected first order, next to a third-order transport ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2659-L2664)).
5. (v) The relation to phase-field methods ([doi:10.1006/jcph.1999.6332](https://doi.org/10.1006/jcph.1999.6332)) and to the CST, the delivery closest to a variational statement ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2666-L2675), [[concepts/integral-surface-tension-cst]]).
6. The record states in two ways which factor of `max|U|(T) = u_0 exp(G)` the pairing attacks. CLAUDE.md says the pairing attacks `u_0` and implicitness attacks `G` ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L462-L465)). The Shannon audit says accuracy sets the level and the pairing sets the sign of the growth budget ([PSH 0f](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/plan-shannon-parasitic-currents.md#L644-L647)). The article claims both an exact equilibrium and no net work ([SL article](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/semi-lagrangian-level-set/sl-level-set-article/semiLagrangianLevelSet.tex#L2591-L2602)). A pre-registered read-out must name its target factor.
7. No read-out is pre-registered. The ladder starts with the exact-solution unit gate and the static gate ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/CLAUDE.md#L474-L497)). The roadmap lists the gates for a new capillary operator: zero force on a plane, cancellation of a constant curvature in the `rAUf` face-flux space, orientation reversal, and polyhedral and decomposed meshes ([RM P3](https://github.com/leia-openfoam/leia/blob/d1e3414/docs/capillary-level-set-research-roadmap.md#L632-L645)).
