Why beta fails — start from the gradient law

Differentiate \(\partial_t\psi + \mathbf u\cdot\nabla\psi = f_{nl}\,\psi\) and evaluate at \(\psi = 0\). With \(g = |\nabla\psi|\) and the interfacial normal strain \(a = \hat n\cdot\nabla\mathbf u\cdot\hat n\):

\[\frac{\mathrm D g}{\mathrm D t} = g\,(f_{nl} - a)\]

R: \(f_{nl} = a\)

\(\mathrm D g/\mathrm D t = 0\) — identically. The source cancels interfacial stretching exactly. There is no target to chase and no fixed point to be unstable about; whatever error remains is numerical, which is why it converges under refinement.

beta: \(f_{nl} = \beta - g\)

\(\mathrm D g/\mathrm D t = g\,(\beta - g - a)\) — a logistic equation in \(g\) with two fixed points:

  • \(g^\ast = \beta - a\) — stable, but set by the flow
  • \(g = 0\) — unstable whenever \(\beta - a > 0\)

Both of them are trouble, and for different reasons.

The same law is why the two sources are not variants of one idea: one sets the right-hand side to zero, the other sets up a competition against the flow.

Failure 1 — the target is set by the flow, so refinement cannot fix it

measured — h-independent Band mean \(|\nabla\psi|\) at maximal deformation, N = 32…512: 1.516, 1.498, 1.478, 1.485, 1.481 — flat to ~2% over a sixteenfold refinement. R over the same ladder: 1.071 → 1.002.

\(g^\ast = \beta - a\) contains no mesh spacing. A discretization residual vanishes as \(h\to0\); a wrong target does not. This is the cleanest discriminator in the record, and it says the offset is structural rather than numerical — so no amount of resolution, and no better limiter, will remove it.

Note what it is not: volume and shape errors sit at \(10^{-5}\)–\(10^{-4}\) for every arm. beta is not displacing the interface. It is holding \(|\nabla\psi|\) at the wrong level.

Failure 2 — over most of the interface the target is unreachable

measured — target negative Band strain reaches \(\max a = 2.77\) against \(\beta = 1\). The target \(\beta - a\) is negative somewhere in the band for 93% of the time steps.

Wherever \(a > \beta\), the stable fixed point \(\beta - a\) is negative — and \(g = |\nabla\psi|\) is a magnitude. It cannot reach it. The logistic dynamics then drive \(g\) toward the only reachable fixed point, \(g = 0\): the level set flattens hardest exactly where the flow stretches hardest.

consequence Band minimum \(|\nabla\psi|\) at N = 512: 0.423 for beta against 0.883 for R — the two seeing the identical flow.

Choosing a bigger \(\beta\) does not escape this: \(a\) is a property of the flow, and on any stretching interface it will exceed whatever constant you pick somewhere.

Failure 3 — \(g=0\) is unstable, so the flattening rebounds

Near \(g = 0\) the logistic equation linearises to \(\mathrm D g/\mathrm D t \approx (\beta - a)\,g\). While \(a > \beta\) that rate is negative and \(g\) collapses. When the strain reverses sign, the rate turns positive and the collapsed cells grow exponentially.

measured — onset at the reversal Band max \(|\nabla\psi|\): 154.9 at t = 1.025 for T = 2, and 98.0 at t = 4.063 for T = 8 — i.e. at t/T = 0.5125 and 0.508. The same fraction of the period, four times the absolute time: the trigger is the reversal, not a clock.

So flattening and blow-up are not two problems. They are one mechanism seen on either side of the reversal: collapse toward an unstable fixed point, then exponential escape from it.

The blow-up is absent on coarse meshes and appears under refinement — between N=128 and 256 at T=2, already at N=128 at T=8, because longer exposure needs less resolution to trigger it. Running the coarse end of a ladder cannot detect this.

bottom line Band gradient error at t=T, N=512: no source 0.0001 (order +2.63), R 0.048 (+0.70), beta 6.897 (−1.24). beta is worse than applying no source at all, and its error grows with resolution. It should not be used.

Retracted: “beta + explicit diverges at every CFL”

retracted — falsified at its own configuration The earlier claim was that beta+explicit blew up at N=128, T=2 for CFL ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1.0} through an anti-damped oscillation, dt-independently. Re-run after the 2026-08 source fixes: all three linearizations complete at every CFL and agree to five significant digits.

The original divergence came from the two defects that branch carried — a reversed sign on the explicit contribution and a use-after-free returning a dangling tmp — not from the relaxation being explicitly lagged. The real reason to reject beta is the mechanism above, and it has nothing to do with the linearization: all three fail identically, because they discretize the same wrong continuum source.

R near the resolution limit: over-flattening

measured verdict — worse than no source Reversed vortex T=8, N=128: volume error 0.488 (R) vs 0.238 (noSource); final band max\(|\nabla\psi|\) = 0.19 — the source kept cancelling "steepening" that was actually the under-resolved filament's discrete structure, flattening the band below usability.

Scope rule: strain cancellation is exact only while the interface is resolved; near \(\kappa h \sim 1\) the reconstructed normal (and hence R) is unreliable. Candidate guard: mollify R by a resolution indicator — open work.

Stack 4 of 5

R controls steepening, not flattening

measured scope limit Band max\(|\nabla\psi|\): 5.5–7.4 → ≤ 1.36 under R (T=8). Band min\(|\nabla\psi|\) → 0 by t ≈ 1 unchanged under R.

Mechanism: R cancels strain-driven drift of BOTH signs along characteristics — so a min\(\to 0\) that survives R is not strain-driven: it is the filament thinning below the mesh (numerical diffusion of a sub-cell sign oscillation). No source term repairs resolution; only refinement does (measured: partial survival at N=128, death at N≤64).

Pitfall: the dead limit entry

measured — bit-identical "clipped" and "unclipped" runs limit is read ONLY by the tanh profile; signedDistance ignores it. Every "clip at 0.1" hypothesis built on that entry tested nothing — two studies compared identical initial fields.

Repair: the entry is now commented "tanh profile ONLY" in every template; lesson: verify that a dictionary entry is actually consumed before building hypotheses on it (grep the reader, or perturb it and diff the output).
