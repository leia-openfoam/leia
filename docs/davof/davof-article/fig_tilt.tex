\caption{A cell $k$ cut by the plane that CCS positions from $\alpha_k$. The plane (red) has the volume $\alpha_k V_k$ below it and pivots about its position $\mathbf{x}_k$ in the cell; the exact interface $\Sigma$ (blue) and the plane cross near this pivot, and a normal turned by $\theta_k$ separates them by about $\theta_k$ times the distance from it, so the plane centroid is within $(h/2)\,\theta_k$ of $\Sigma$. The volume flux of the face $f$ is the liquid volume in the swept slab (grey); the sliver between the plane and $\Sigma$ inside the slab (orange) is the error of the flux of one step, of the relative size $\mathrm{Co}\,\theta_k$ of the cell volume. The tilt is drawn at $10^\circ$ for visibility.}
